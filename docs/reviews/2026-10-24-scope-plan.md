## Scope decisions

- Preserve stable residents, workplaces/leisure, persistent cars, parked locations and citywide traffic. Subway is an efficient abstract middle leg, not a replacement for persistent traffic.
- One supported runtime path, automatic supported-business enrollment, internal balance values and a clean development-save baseline. Drop experimental migrations; retain current-version save protection and automatic game/RLO readiness checks.
- Represent two total economic consumers per physical resident initially; consider three after measuring two. Own wallets, meal intentions and native receipts; share valid travel information and base routines. No multiplied sale, wallet, service capacity or traffic.
- Additional consumers use small saved meal excursions on the existing scheduler. Completed idle consumers can resynchronize at the anchor's next safe home stay as an explicit abstraction of non-food travel. That never completes an active trip, settles uncertain payment or moves a physical person/car.
- Use simple worker/seeking chances and declared income/support. Remove resident departure/replacement churn. Native payroll, family simulation, rival finances, custom logistics, more categories and elaborate transport/social scenes are outside this release.
- Native stock, service, supplier deliveries and business accounts remain authoritative. Whole-business ownership prevents duplicate customer generation; unsupported menus return to native ownership through a safe handoff. Stockouts/sickness do not create replacement native demand.
