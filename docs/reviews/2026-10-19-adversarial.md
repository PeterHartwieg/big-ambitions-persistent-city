# Adversarial isolated-Mac campaign — 19 October 2026

This campaign attempted to break the accepted physical prototype through actual game controls, body retirement/reacquisition, native speed changes, repeated loads and native activities. It applied **no production source, package or RLO changes**. Raw saves, crash diagnostics and requests remain local in ignored `build/adversarial-validation/`.

Tested Persistent City **0.4.0/schema 5**, DLL SHA-256 `96ad442703672a682b0305df0e408a0598acdd40b1c5edfe367b93a4c1b560b1`, Big Ambitions build 3682, RLO 0.40.4, isolated `agentmac` runtime 6 in **background/Metal** mode on port 28330. The normal Mac profile and Windows were not controlled. Ownership was `persistent-city-adversarial-sol-20261019`.

## Findings

**P1 impact, attribution unresolved: one fatal native renderer crash.** After an actual player collapse, hospital time skip, native hospital exit and a bridge clock-rate change back to 1, the process terminated with SIGSEGV at 17:26:32 UTC. The managed crash stack is `ScriptableRenderContext.Internal_Cull → HDRP → Camera.Render → RealLifeOverhaul.Flare.FlareCityCamera.Render → FlareDirector.PhotoTake`. This establishes a real failure of the tested Mac configuration, **not that Persistent City caused it**. The preceding RLO log reported a portrait-camera timeout. No Persistent City method appears on the captured managed stack, and there was no preceding Persistent City runtime-failed diagnostic. A restart of the unchanged installation, loading the owned hospital checkpoint and repeating exit/clock-rate controls, did not reproduce it; three subsequent same-session reloads and native activities also completed. Do not turn the earlier private-prototype approval into a claim of crash-free Mac rendering. Evidence: `crash1-Player-agent.log`, `crash1.ips`, `crash-repro-console.jsonl`, `response-adv_crash_repro_*.json`. A next investigation should isolate the RLO camera/native renderer interaction before selecting any source fix; no fix was attempted in this campaign.

**Limited availability observation, not an established product defect:** `queue_002`–`queue_023` recorded zero active taxis against replenishment target three for 124.43 real seconds, with ordinary physical traffic running. This occurred at the artificial **bridge clock 6/native physics 1** combination. Later clock-1/native-1 samples had two or three taxis against target three. No taxi ride was attempted in the zero interval. This can be a native-clock stress effect or replenishment-policy issue; the data do not establish a standard-control taxi failure. See the native counters in the corresponding responses.

**Parent assessment after the completed report:** the captured crash warrants investigation, but the stack alone neither proves nor excludes an interaction with Persistent City's scene objects. No targeted production fix is justified without isolating that interaction. Source review confirms taxi replenishment is a target: `PhysicalTrafficHooks.SelectIdle` permits taxis below it while retaining the native group draw and spawn opportunities; it does not directly maintain a guaranteed live count. The README now states that limit. The high missed-activity count and temporary simulation lag are useful improvement leads, not proof of corrupted state; classify misses by route, opening window and travel delay before changing scheduling policy. Add same-frame time-machine/interior context to future diagnostic snapshots to remove the separate-frame ambiguity observed here.

**Test-harness correction:** later bridge `time` calls that omitted `keep_needs` reset it to false. The player consequently collapsed during the long traffic run. This was not a Persistent City teleport or failure. Fixed-camera traffic analysis uses only `queue_000`–`queue_023`; `queue_024` onward is the native hospital/time-skip interruption. The unfortunately named owned checkpoint `FT Adversarial Sol Congestion` is the **hospital** checkpoint; `FT Adversarial Sol Occupied` is the earlier traffic checkpoint. Subsequent time controls explicitly carried needs protection.

## Limits

No Windows, populated-business economy, dynamic live road-closure mutation, complete visible end-to-end journey, representative FPS/CPU/memory benchmark or user fun judgment is established. No positive RLO reservation case was captured. Frozen venue/assignment reachability, conservative car admission, virtual parking and background native fast-forward remain the agreed policies; high missed-activity counts alone do not make those policies a new implementation defect. The two-day run does not test minimum-seven-day resident departures. This campaign does not justify public-release approval.
