## Ownership

RLO provides an immutable donor catalogue of identity/persona/appearance information. Eligible rentable/buyable residential interiors set the population independently. Provisional per-unit occupant bands are 0 below 20 m², 1 below 40 m², 2 below 75 m², 4 below 150 m², 5 below 300 m² and 7 thereafter. Whole-property area never substitutes for missing interior area. Large villas remain bounded, while population scale follows the actual eligible unit inventory. The fresh-city target is 90% of aggregate occupant capacity, not a fraction of occupied units. These allocations do not establish family relationships or staff roles. Additional residents receive persistent mod-owned identities remixed from those donors. Persistent City owns assignments, daily routines, logical locations, route progress and its save schema. Thin adapters read the game and RLO; rules remain testable without Unity. An authoritative population must eventually arbitrate visible actors, transport and social meetings so one person never appears twice.

The population/traffic development candidate is 0.6.2, schema 8; current build evidence belongs in README.md and CHECKS.md. Its 335-interior reference inventory gives 1,064 maximum occupants and a fresh target of 957. Housing migration retains existing occupants even where they exceed corrected limits or the fresh target. The historical 25,495-resident whole-property experiment is superseded. Earlier native background validation is recorded in [the interior report](reviews/2026-10-19-interiors.md); it does not validate physical actors.

The physical milestone retains the native economy's customers and workers. A shop visit is an activity, not a transaction. Fares influence native-adapter route choice but debit nobody. Each allocated private car has a persisted model, paint seed and logical curb location, and follows saved road legs. Nearby travellers receive owned native bodies. RLO scenes, service vehicles and stationary special-purpose actors remain explicit exceptions; social/casualty bodies are observed read-only, never taken over. Virtual parking was explicitly accepted for this first version; visible parking manoeuvres, spaces, boarding animations and train interiors remain deferred.

## Agreed economy roadmap — 21 October 2026

The existing identity, routine, journey and physical-population simulation is the foundation. The next objective is: residents choose where to buy food, the player can see why, and those choices can determine actual shop sales. Complete that small loop before expanding the scope.

1. **Prove meaningful food demand.** Use one food category and a small set of real competing businesses. Read current business information, model recurring food needs and explicitly temporary spending allowances, and retain purchase intentions and outcomes. Prices, opening hours, availability and travel must explain choices and unmet demand. A small citizen/shop insight view distinguishes simulated choices from observed native activity. In the same milestone, establish how native purchasing, inventory and accounting can execute the eventual sales. Keep shadow mode bounded: its exit is understandable behaviour and credible demand scale, not exact reproduction of vanilla sales.
2. **Make the retail slice authoritative.** Persistent City owns customer decisions only within the selected scope and replaces overlapping native demand there. Reuse native sale execution and bookkeeping wherever practicable. Keep this pilot opt-in and reversible, with sustained play/performance evidence before expanding it.
3. **Close the household money loop.** Actual employment and wages fund household spending and essential expenses. Replace temporary allowances before adding many more needs; those allowances are an explicit external source of purchasing power, not earned income. Existing staffing retains authority until the corresponding integration is deliberately introduced.
4. **Connect demand to supply and business viability.** Add replenishment, production/imports, operating costs and rival finances. Businesses should respond to shortages, prices and competition. Recheck native rival accounting when reaching this stage rather than assuming historical estimates are real cash balances.
5. **Broaden the changing city economy.** Expand categories and let financial outcomes drive hiring, investment, capacity, business openings and closures.

### Small shops and useful expansion — 22 October 2026

Peter's design target: a well-chosen 15-capacity shop should be a worthwhile first business, with a second small location a credible growth choice. Expansion should be able to reach additional customers and serve previously unmet demand, without automatically taking the first shop's business. This remains a target for the live economy. The routine/service experiment below demonstrates the local-coverage mechanism; native single-shop sales are now verified, while live expansion economics and profitability remain unvalidated.

Peter clarified that every business option need not be viable. Different locations, offers and prices should suit different customers and budgets; an unsuitable shop can attract few customers or none. There are no minimum sales, owner exemptions or automatic rescue adjustments. Home district may shift the distribution of buying power while retaining substantial individual variation. Personality can change willingness to buy prepared food; neither personality nor district gives every resident the same spending limit. Temporary allowances remain explicit policy assumptions until household income and expenses replace them.

Demand should arise around residents' homes, workplaces and daily routes at the times they need something. A convenient small shop can serve that local demand; a larger shop elsewhere does not automatically attract it. Capacity constrains how much service a shop can deliver. It does not guarantee customers or make size alone a reason to choose that shop. Native source inspection maps building capacity to an hourly customer ceiling; effective staffed and sellable throughput still needs observation before live integration.

A second shop should earn its place by covering another local market or relieving demand the first cannot serve in time. Nearby identical locations can still overlap; customer choice should follow the same rules regardless of ownership. The intended result is meaningful location choice, not guaranteed sales or an exemption from competition for the player's chain. A larger single shop remains useful where demand is concentrated. Small-shop viability also depends on setup and operating costs, so sales alone will not establish profitability.

The controlled routine/service comparison covers one 15-capacity shop, two 15-capacity shops serving different local markets, and one 30-capacity shop in a single location, keeping population, needs and spending power fixed. Track total fulfilled needs, each shop's sales, unmet demand, travel and service delays; assess profitability when verified costs are available. The useful expansion case should show the second location serving additional demand while the first retains a viable role. Include overlapping locations as a contrast so the result demonstrates the value of coverage rather than simply rewarding shop count. This extends the food milestone's local-demand and service checks; it does not require implementing the whole business-finance loop first.

## Continuity rules

- A route is contiguous and ends at a known destination. Unreachable routes wait and retry; arrival is never fabricated.
- Player/camera position must change only representation quality, never determine which citizen exists or what they did.
- Travel completes before another activity can start. Late/closed arrivals may lose an activity; they do not teleport to recover a timetable.
- Personal quiet hours affect optional outings; work/study and committed journeys/waits retain priority. Home spans cross midnight. Commute preparation is staggered and stores a duration estimate; departure always selects a fresh route. See the [daily-rhythm policy and checks](reviews/2026-10-19-daily-rhythms.md).
- An attainable later opening uses a persisted waiting state with the original destination and activity deadline. It never replaces that deadline with a return-home wake time. Waiting is at the current physical entrance; admission is recorded separately.
- A simulation step has a bounded event budget and records how far it has caught up. A snapshot between simultaneous events is valid and deterministic.
- Keep the immutable RLO source roster separate from active residents. Departures happen only at home, release simulated assignments and allocate a nonreused mod-owned identity remixed from source donors. Source profiles remain in RLO.
- Choose work and leisure with a seeded distance bias, allowing farther attractive venues. Daily leisure choices remain fixed once a trip or waiting period begins.
- Persist actual assignments and journeys. Reject incompatible snapshots instead of re-rolling people or discarding newer fields. Housing-policy migrations preserve existing occupants and physical events, and cannot silently shrink an experimental legacy population.
- Native housing inputs use only known rentable/buyable interior area and current access checks. Gated private estates remain excluded regardless of player ownership; fixed AI villas and special-rival homes receive read-only catalogue filters. The reference Hamptons residences are currently excluded by their gates.
- A renderer reports physical delays back to the simulation. Arrival uses the normal admission queue only after measured motion reaches the route end. Small native avoidance detours withhold unproven progress while continuing the already validated corner path; they do not permanently latch a stop flag.
- Road presentation requires the persisted topology fingerprint and directed node sequence. No current waypoint match means no body lease; it never fabricates a road route or borrows an active foreign vehicle. Actual native corner evidence gates segment completion, including stop lines and multiple physics steps between rendered frames.
- Pausing retains physical bodies. Time-machine skips and interiors release them before background catch-up. Native 6× also releases owned bodies and continues background travel after the game's normal traffic clear; normal speed reacquires eligible bodies. Unloading restores native pause/spawn behaviour.
- One later population ID maps Flare, social contacts, employees, scene actors and passengers. The prototype currently imports Flare only and claims no such unification.

## Release gates

Background checks: cadence-independent results, save/reload midjourney, backward-clock rejection, unreachable destinations, closed and overnight venues, malformed/future saves, bounded event processing and dependency refusal.

Visible walking gate: follow a selected citizen for a complete itinerary; leave and meet them elsewhere; reload midwalk; verify appearance; switch between street, interior and free camera; saturate the actor budget without visible disappearance; remove the mod and verify ambient spawning restores.

Transport gate: follow one person from home through each mode to a destination, with capacity and delayed arrivals; preserve vehicle ownership while distant; account for parked locations and mode transfers; no duplicate drivers/passengers.

Performance gate: measure actual main-thread time, path requests, appearance-build spikes, allocations, memory over a long session and time-skip catch-up on the target game/modpack. Synthetic results are only regression evidence.

Fun gate requires playtesting: familiar neighbours, understandable destinations, varied but consistent routines, readable rush hours, useful citizen inspection and limited obstruction of normal play. It cannot be established by unit tests.

### Automatic recurring meals — 22 October follow-up

Peter authorized the next gate after the native connection pilot: ordinary recurring intentions, one scheduler-owned journey, durable purchase ownership and scoped replacement of generated native customers. Implemented in the optional saved food scope; [current verification](reviews/2026-10-22-food-routine-sales.md) records 24 natural purchases over three native days, four reload checkpoints and duplicate-sale prevention. Start with one stocked product at selected player full-service shops, keeping restocking and daily accounting native. The next economy step is household income and expenses, followed by broader products/supply. Normal-play performance and broader activation remain separate gates. Do not add guaranteed viability, owner protection or minimum sales.

Peter then challenged the eight-sales-per-day result and asked to account for 1,000+ citizens. The [demand audit](reviews/2026-10-22-food-demand-audit.md) identifies compressed travel time and the mandatory separate return trip as the main constraints. It adds modest, stable break flexibility and reduces repeated catalogue/choice/path work. Before household finance expansion, examine buying during an existing shopping stop and short on-route takeaway detours. Keep the same resident/journey ownership, next-commitment deadline, finite service/stock and event budgets. Do not compensate by silently multiplying citizens, inventing external customers or extending every break for hours.

Peter subsequently authorized testing a citywide one-third calendar speed, based on Driven Rides' travel-time experience. This experiment now precedes additional demand tuning: preserve movement/physics pace and game-time schedules, prices, allowances, service capacity and deadlines, while each day provides three times as much wall-clock travel time. Make the factor configurable, coordinate Driven Rides so its ride multiplier does not compound, preserve time-machine durations, and restore ordinary clock ownership on unload or failure. Compare the same disposable checkpoint for a full game day at factors 1 and 1/3, recording sales, queues, completed journeys, missed activities and CPU work. The PC preparation was stopped at Peter's request and testing moved to the isolated Mac rig. Native results belong in the dated clock-pace review; no normal-profile deployment is authorized by this test.

The [completed Mac comparison](reviews/2026-10-22-city-clock.md) supports retaining one third as the candidate baseline: the same shop serves 13→42 customers per game day, and more shopping/leisure visits complete. Missed-activity counts also rise, so the next implementation should investigate destination reachability and opening-window fit before connecting existing shopping stops to purchases. The real taxi compatibility check passes without compounded slowdown. Native performance with rendered population remains unverified.
Peter authorized continuing with destination feasibility on 23 October, with the PC available for validation. New optional shopping/leisure decisions should check an actual route and useful opening time before departure, try a small number of alternatives, and retain the original activity deadline.
