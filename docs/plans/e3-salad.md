## Conditional reader and stop decision

[SaladFeasibilityReader.Read(existingRegistration)](../../Runtime/SaladFeasibilityReader.cs) uses the existing [DirectStockShowcaseReader](../../Runtime/DirectStockShowcaseReader.cs) through a private-constructor pinned salad descriptor. Call explicitly on the Unity main thread, using an existing registration; reselect after reload. There is no arbitrary public product selector, new holder abstraction, native setup, bridge command, hook, poll, timer, city scan, normal-play caller or persistent native-data cache. Without evidence of a different holder contract, broadening the reader would manufacture support. Donut/cupcake facades and every read branch/refusal/output remain unchanged.

The conditional hypothesis is **finished salad cargo on a loaded `ShowcaseShelf` whose whitelist contains the exact salad ID**, with consuming resources and no converted/shared inputs or outputs. The holder name is copied from observed definitions, never a name-based authorization for `saladbar`/`woodensaladbar`. Non-showcase employee workstations remain refused even with same-ID cargo. Ingredients/intermediates and storage never count as finished salad. A rig finding of a non-showcase holder keeps this probe unsupported and requires new evidence and review before any adapter extension.

Bounds and semantics are unchanged: at most 2,000 furniture and 128 menu/price/cargo/producer/whitelist/tag entries; exact price; detached read-only equipment list; `DirectStockFieldsVerified` means conservative field shape only. `Eligibility` is always Unknown. `RawProductUnits` is direct showcase cargo; `RawBagUnits`/`RawCounters` form one unqualified business-wide pool, never service capacity, available meals or reservations. Closed/nonplayer/wrong business shape, missing/ambiguous catalogue or price, invalid money, mismatched whitelist, malformed/oversized/shared/nested cargo, workstation/converted resources, shared outputs, counter/product overlap, missing/nonbag counter stock and overflow refuse. Known zero stock/bags remain zero. Missing bag cargo refuses. No atomicity across concurrent native mutation is promised.

**Outcome: documented stop at the unestablished shipped holder contract.** The conditional reader is a future bridge comparison tool, not proof that a native salad shop can sell. No checkout seam is added: `ProcessCustomer` creates random data and other writes; the existing `NativeFoodMarket.Buy` constructs a customer/order and is restricted to the current three products. The rig must observe natural native salad checkout and native daily settlement only; no synthetic customers, cargo, recipes, orders or money.
