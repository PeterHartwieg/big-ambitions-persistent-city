## Proposed data contract: population schema 17

**A2 status (30 October, offline only):** implemented on `codex/pc-a2-schema-traits` (stacked on A1). 17 was confirmed free (current was 16). Shape choices made in implementation: traits are stored as `{Bands, Version}`, one digit 0–4 per trait in fixed version-1 order, rather than ten named fields, which saves 174 characters per record. `Today` is null until a consumer's first outcome and records how many leading meals of its day ended before the upgrade (`Unrecorded`). Each outcome is one compact string on the wire and stores its recording minute plus the checkout minute, both as whole minutes; only a purchase names its shop and product. A2 has no separate decision time to store. The decision snapshot also captures opening hours, service refusal, customer-service and district-demand evidence, with unknown states. Each shortlisted shop records the progress of its outward and return quote slots (unrequested, answered, refused, not needed), with a cursor, so a reload resumes at the next slot. Loyalty/curiosity is generated but never described. No config keys were added because nothing reads choice tunables until A3. Legacy choice and economy are unchanged against the schema-16 baseline. Evidence: [CHECKS](../../CHECKS.md#step-a2-population-schema-17-and-lifestyle-traits---30-october-2026-offline-only).

Use **17**, the next version after the inspected 16. Reconfirm immediately before implementation; if another feature has consumed it, coordinate the next version rather than overwrite that contract. Keep the namespace/key `persistentcity:state_v1`; the embedded schema distinguishes formats. The detached `FoodShadowState.CurrentSchema = 5` is a different document and need not change for A.

## Deterministic draws and reload contract

Reuse `StableRandom` in `Core/Population.cs`, including its incremental prefix hashing; do not use `System.Random`, Unity random state, process hash codes, wall time, frame number, pool generation, camera position or enumeration order. `Core/DestinationChoice.cs` already demonstrates a seeded exponential race in which all eligible places compete; it is an algorithm pattern, not today's food hook.

Define canonical versioned keys with escaped/length-delimited string components and invariant decimal integers:

- Trait draw: `pc:traits:v1 | stableResidentOrConsumerId | traitCode`, seeded by **saved `CityState.Seed`**. Each trait gets its own domain. Do not alter RLO source identities or hash mutable name, address, job, body or settings into traits.
- Shop shortlist draw: `pc:food-shortlist:v1 | consumerId | day | mealKind | logicalContextKey | businessId`.
- Offer/outside draw: `pc:food-choice:v1 | consumerId | day | mealKind | logicalContextKey | attemptOrdinal | businessId | productId`; use a reserved, noncolliding outside-option code for household food.

The context is a saved logical stay occurrence: origin place/activity and admission/capture event minute, plus its continuation deadline. Derive it at the simulation event (`RecentVisits` / `FoodRoutineStop.CapturedAt`), then latch it. Do not derive it from when Update happens to observe the resident. Same-context deferrals, cache misses, planner yields and reloads reuse the key and ordinal. Different valid stops may reconsider a missed/declined meal within the bounded attempt policy; a paid meal or uncertain receipt cannot.

For a candidate weight `w > 0`, map a hash to `u = (h + 1) / (2^32 + 2)` and rank `-log(u) / w`, breaking exact ties by ordinal business/product IDs. This follows the established `DestinationChoice` convention. Pin test vectors, coefficient tables and clamps under policy version 1; normalize weights to finite positive ranges and prevent zero/underflow, NaN and overflow. Extremely distant feasible offers still get a representable positive weight. Test numerical agreement on the native runtime; cross-runtime logarithm differences near ties are a risk, so define a stable quantization/tie rule if native vectors diverge. Distribution tests across seeds must not require a particular winner for every seed.

Traits and existing saved choices survive clock-factor, optional-stop-percentage, render-budget and UI settings changes. These settings are absent from the draw key. A genuine clock change can change travel feasibility and schedules under existing retiming rules; it does not authorize rerolling a saved decision. Changed prices, stock, gates or player actions may invalidate service, not select a new lucky replacement at checkout.

Seeded RNG alone does **not** establish identical native replay: decision supply is currently a transient per-native-minute cache, quote geometry can be invalidated, and optional-stop feasibility includes unsaved sampled geometry. For A:

1. Commit the small logical decision header/shortlist and captured native input image before yielding work. A save taken mid-decision must serialize that coherent image, completed quote slots and the next pending slot; reload must not silently refill it from a warmer/cooler decision cache. Keep only immutable values, never Unity objects. Save barriers must also retain any validated route geometry needed for commitment, under the existing 512-leg limit.
2. If a food event becomes resumable, use a private working transaction and a serialized continuation; reserve stock, change `ResidentMeal`, cancel stops and write outcomes only at commit. Extend the save synchronizer and snapshot validation accordingly. `CloneForEvent` / `CommitEvent` need explicit trait handling for resident working copies. Never publish half a sampled decision or retry ordinal.
3. Restore captured choices without drawing again. Revalidate navigation/authority and live checkout as required for safety. Changed external state causes an explicit interruption/refusal; it never makes the old decision into a different purchase. Before a capture exists, replay from the same saved world and logical event must materialize the same input values regardless of frame slicing/cache warmth; add an invariant test and native checkpoint for this, including supply-cache reconstruction. If that fails, move capture to the deterministic event boundary; do not waive it as “seeded”.

Acceptance is identical A traits, shortlist, draws, chosen/no-purchase result and reasons under unchanged saved inputs, plus no duplicate money or orders across interruption. Do not claim this retroactively fixes unrelated native physical timing or optional-stop geometry nondeterminism. New replay dependencies must be made explicit in the decision image; an unbounded full-day world snapshot is out of scope.

Proposed `FoodChoicePolicy` consumes a bounded immutable evaluated-offer list and `ResidentTraits`; it returns selected IDs/outside choice plus reason/factors. `FoodCommerce` supplies city seed, stable opportunity/context and policy version. The live flow is:

Use the existing supported, whole-owned `FoodScope` catalogue (save limit 1,024), cached entrance geometry and current authority, before reading detailed supply. A fixed small top-eight buffer avoids sorting/allocating an entire city catalogue. Version cached catalogue images on business/menu/ownership or entrance changes.
2. **Bounded native reads:** materialize at most eight shops, reusing `NativeFoodMarket`'s minute supply cache. Keep unsupported shops outside the ownership contract. Cheap factual closed/stock/staff/affordability checks still prune route work. Unknown evidence stays unknown.
3. **Existing route stage:** at most outward plus return for each of those eight shops, through `WalkingQuote` and the current validity checks. No route search in trait generation, shortlist weighting, final choice or inspector. No second search to explain a losing offer. Preserve existing cache validation, public access and geometry provenance.
4. **Remove preference-as-feasibility cutoffs only for new-policy food trips:** remove the tolerance filters in `ReadLocal`, `ReadLocalBusinesses` and `Decide`. Do not globally change `TravelPreferences` or commute routing. Supply a finite food-specific search allowance large enough to cover the route that could physically fit the opportunity's remaining time, derived from the planner's walking pace; preserve the outward/return total-time check. Carry this allowance consistently through return quotation and `IWalkingQuoteValidity`; a saved return must not be rejected by accidentally reinstating the old personal tolerance. The personal willingness-to-travel trait affects weight, never eligibility. No car/subway food expansion is proposed.
5. **Feasibility then sample:** retain complete outward/return paths, openings, queue/service capacity, meal window/deadline, stock and exact affordability as hard facts. Over-budget offers remain impossible; willingness to pay affects the chance among affordable offers. “Distant never ruled out” means distance preference is never a cutoff: an actually unreachable shop or a trip unable to fit its return still cannot serve. A shop outside today's sampled eight was not evaluated, not rejected as unreachable. Every otherwise eligible shop can enter a shortlist across draws; this bounded approximation is not an exhaustive all-shop multinomial.
6. **Positive offer weights:** derive finite positive weights from taste, price sensitivity, quality evidence, travel willingness/distance, queue impatience and current time pressure, retaining meal fit and the known district/service signals. Prefer a documented bounded rational/table mapping, with explicit positive floors, over unbounded score exponentiation. Every coefficient comes from the [config tunables](#choice-tunables-config) (decision 3), clamped to its range. Stronger taste or nearer distance raises relative chance, holding other inputs fixed; neither guarantees a purchase. The funded household/prepared-food alternative competes as its own positive-weight option when valid. Never invent a free outside meal or use personality to create money.
7. **Commit once:** the selected existing route, exact price and service terms feed today's normal `TryBeginMealVisit` / represented excursion / reservation path. At an existing shop, evaluate its menu versus the outside option with zero travel, without expanding the visit into another shopping trip. Capture a factual reason (“chose burgers; likes them”, “chose household food”, “queue would not fit break”, “no confirmed route”) and at most two factors, without pretending randomness had one exclusive cause.

### Choice tunables (config)

Decision 3 places every choice weight and temperature in `config/persistent-city.json`, read by `PrototypeSettings` like the existing settings. Values outside a range are clamped to it and logged once; missing keys take the default. Defaults are starting points for A3 response-curve tuning, not frozen policy. Tunables are not part of any draw key: a committed decision is saved and never re-rolled when a value changes; only later, uncommitted decisions use the new values. Under unchanged config, a reload replays identically.

| Key | Default | Range | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FoodChoiceTemperature` | 1.0 | 0.25–4.0 | Divides every log-weight; lower is greedier, higher closer to uniform. |
| `FoodChoiceWeightFloor` | 0.02 | 0.001–0.25 | Minimum relative weight of any feasible offer, so nothing reaches zero. |
| `FoodChoiceTasteWeight` | 1.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of loves/dislikes product bands. |
| `FoodChoicePriceWeight` | 1.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of price sensitivity among affordable offers. |
| `FoodChoiceDistanceWeight` | 1.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of travel time against willingness to travel. |
| `FoodChoiceDistanceScaleMinutes` | 10 | 2–60 | Walking minutes at which distance halves weight for a neutral traveller. |
| `FoodChoiceQueueWeight` | 1.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of expected wait against patience. |
| `FoodChoiceQualityWeight` | 0.5 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of known quality evidence against quality focus. |
| `FoodChoiceTimePressureWeight` | 1.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Strength of remaining break time against time pressure. |
| `FoodChoiceHealthWeight` | 0.0 | 0.0–3.0 | Health tags; stays 0 until a reviewed product tag table exists (decision 2). |
| `FoodChoiceHouseholdWeight` | 1.0 | 0.1–5.0 | Relative weight of the funded household-food alternative. |
| `FoodShortlistTemperature` | 1.0 | 0.25–4.0 | Spread of the all-shop weighted shortlist draw; distance never excludes. |

Shortlist size 8 (decision 5) and 4 contexts per meal (decision 6) are fixed bounds, not tunables: they cap route work and saved state.

The route-call **ceiling remains 16 outward/return requests for a full eight-shop decision**, with existing return revalidation accounted separately. That is a cap, not an assertion that realized calls or expensive path lengths cannot increase: replacing local cutoffs can change both. Added features must not add route passes per offer or per trait. A deterministic pruning bound may reject only a trip proven unable to fit; never label straight-line geometry as a route. Do not fill a rejected shortlist by scanning/querying another eight, which would silently multiply the cap.

All three live entry paths (`Decide`, `DecideRepresented` via `Decide`, `DecideAtStop`) must share the policy/context latch. `ObserveStop` retains its ability to offer a new context after a decline. Food retains priority over optional presentation: preserve `CancelOptionalStop` and paired cancellation semantics, but cancellation and outcome capture must not happen speculatively during a yielded event. `OptionalStopPolicy` keeps its own seed domain and conservation identity; A neither consumes nor transfers optional chances to buy food.

`CitizenData.IsPriceAcceptable` stays the existing exact approved-price bridge. `RetailBusinessSimulator` remains the native stock/order/accounting executor. `NativeFoodMarket.Buy`, `FoodSaleReceipts`, `HouseholdFinance` hold/refuse/confirm and `MealStatus.Uncertain` remain the final safety boundaries. Never write daily revenue directly or retry a failed/uncertain native checkout by rerolling food. No new rival reads/writes in A; a future provider can supply read-only alternative descriptions through an explicit seam, without receiving any native settlement capability.

## Inspector UI and resident selection

**Verified reusable native pieces:** the resident's existing `ThirdPersonCharacter`/capsule/transform, the active body's stable identity/generation, game place/address/district data from `GamePlaces`, and `LocalizorManager` via the panel's `LocalizedName`. **Verified UI implementation in this repository:** the Unity IMGUI overlay in `PopulationRuntime.OnGUI`; it is mod UI, not a verified native citizen-profile window.

No inspected source exposes a safe native resident detail panel that accepts a `pc:<sequence>` identity. RLO's Flare profiles are an immutable donor catalogue, not these generated residents. `Compatibility/RloPhysicalReservations.cs` documents why names/social indices alone cannot identify a person. Do not open or overwrite a donor's Flare profile to impersonate the clicked resident.

Decided implementation (decision 1): **no inspect mode**. In the normal top-down view, a Runtime-only `TryPickResident` seam on `PhysicalPopulation` runs one bounded screen-space capsule/projection pass over active own person bindings, with visibility checks and deterministic nearest-hit tie handling. Picking is lazy: it runs only when the cursor has moved, at most once per throttle interval, and never while the cursor is still and the popup closed. The hovered walker is highlighted; a click on a highlighted walker opens a small popup. A raycast may resolve occlusion, but is not a navigation search. ## Performance requirement and engineering budget

The acceptance target remains [the bounded plan's performance criteria](../implementation-plan.md): combined mod work **p95 ≤ 5 ms per normal-play frame**, no repeated mod-attributable >50 ms stalls; whole-frame p95 within 10% of the matched reference and no new recurring mod-attributable >100 ms hitches. Startup/reload and skip waits have separately frozen ceilings. Include A's UI in mod-work measurement. Inclusive/nested timings must not be added together. These are requirements, not a pass claimed by this document or the historical 29 October sample.

Provisional engineering allocations, to validate on the reference PC: shortlist + traits projection + sampling ≤0.20 ms for a full decision; selected-view refresh/picking ≤0.20 ms on its event frame, closed inspector approximately zero added work. Treat these as sub-budgets inside the total, **not** an additional allowance above 5 ms. If the standing baseline has insufficient headroom, optimize or amortize the responsible work and record the result; do not silently relax the gate.

- Generate/migrate traits once in the existing pure initialization work, not per frame. Cache immutable policy/hash prefixes; avoid LINQ sorts, per-offer key strings and formatting in hot loops.
- Candidate ranking is O(S × 8), S ≤ 1,024 scopes, with O(8) working slots; offer evaluation is O(8 × 4). Avoid O(residents × shops) work each Update. Candidate metadata is lifecycle-cached, detailed supply is per-minute-cached, shared quotes remain bounded to 512. Route and supply input invalidations must never choose a fresh draw by accident.
- If the bounded food event still exceeds the advance budget, extend food planning with a continuation following the existing resident planner pattern and `FoodRouteBatch.Step`'s bounded-query pattern. `FoodRouteBatch` itself is a detached helper with nearest-candidate/tolerance rules, so it cannot be wired in unchanged. Yield between metadata/quote units, hold the logical event minute, keep fixed buffers and save its coherent continuation. One nonpreemptible native NavMesh query may overrun a soft budget; report its cost rather than claim a hard 2 ms ceiling.
- Current food events bypass `CitySimulator.PendingEvent`. The continuation change must include tie ordering with citizen/traffic/budget events, `IsCaughtUpTo`, snapshot publication and interrupted-save tests. Preserve the rule that checkout completes atomically on the main thread. Do not yield inside native sale/receipt processing.
- Do not refill the sampled eight after failed feasibility. Do not add navmesh access for UI, traits, weights, explanations, market statistics or deferred B–D seams.
- Measure both total work and lag/catch-up throughput. Making frames short by indefinitely delaying meals is a failure. Record per-decision route calls and path-query duration as well as p50/p95/max, allocations/GC when available, queue/backlog, pending decision count and payload growth.

Re-measure as each PR-sized slice lands; do not defer all measurement to the end. The separate active performance session is not used or disturbed by this plan. Future rig work requires its own scheduled ownership and authorization.

## Risks and mitigation

- **Distant-shop promise versus finite work:** all-shop cheap weighted admission plus a fixed eight-shop evaluation is an approximation. It removes hard distance exclusions without unbounded routes, but sometimes misses a feasible nearby shop. Show observed reasons truthfully; Q2 is decided (all-shop weighted shortlist of 8).
- **Replay depends on input capture:** supply-cache warmth, route invalidation, event rescheduling and speculative side effects can change outcomes even with identical hashes. Save coherent decision evidence, pin context, preserve native safety refusals, and require pre-capture/mid-capture native replay proof. This is the largest technical risk.
- **Frame cost can move into long paths or saves:** same query count does not imply same milliseconds. Profile distant paths, payload growth, current synchronous food events and inspector refresh; introduce coherent continuation where necessary. Do not use the 2 ms advance budget as proof of the 5 ms combined gate.
- **Strict schema regressions:** current schema-specific shape arithmetic/resolver caches are easy to break. Maintain exact per-version contracts and refusal fixtures, include all new nested-record budgets, preserve source on every failed read/write.
- **Inspector identity/input:** native profile APIs are unverified; donor names and body references are unsafe identifiers. Use own bindings plus generation and stable-ID selection. Hover picking adds per-frame cost; keep it lazy and throttled, and measure closed/hover/open on the rig.
- **Misleading traits or explanations:** future loyalty/brand behavior, provisional health tags and represented consumers can imply simulation that does not exist. Keep phrases factual, avoid numerical optimization readouts and separate observed outcomes from explanatory preferences. Expose no free aggregate research.
- **Economic balance changes:** replacing a minimum with weighted choice and an outside alternative will change demand and business outcomes. Judge controlled response curves and conservation, not guaranteed sales or a chosen winner. No economy rebalancing, richer menus or memory creep in A.
- **Parallel development drift:** other development/performance fixes may land after this baseline. Recheck schema, save contracts, planner budgets and native hooks against the then-current development head before implementing. Use normal merges/review; never operate on the concurrently running rig session's worktree or processes.
