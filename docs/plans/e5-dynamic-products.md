### Loaded definitions first; complete shop contract second

`ItemsGetter.AllItems` is actually an **`IEnumerable<Item>` view of private `ItemsDictionary.Values`**, not a public dictionary or an item-load event. `OnItemsLoaded(IList<Item>)` clears/repopulates that dictionary, adds registered `ModItemsDictionary` entries and keeps vanilla on a name collision. `RegisterModItem(Item)` can add a definition later; `UnregisterModItem(string)` can remove one. PC only observes these methods and loaded fields; it never calls registration, repairs caches or manufactures items. `GetByName(string, bool)` with suppressed errors is a lookup only, but must be guarded by catalogue readiness.

Build a bounded, detached, ordinal-name-keyed catalogue image after load/registration changes. Match each actual advertised ID and each holder/cargo ID to that image and, at capture/checkout, to the current loaded definition. Neither `ItemNameLegacyMap`, the E3 descriptors, config, an API registration, `defaultShelfItemName`, an equipment's name nor `itemsToProduce` alone establishes eligibility. The same rule applies to vanilla and mod namespaces; `ba:` is not privileged.

The following is the **safe direct-stock contract to reuse and prove in loaded play**, not a claim of completed E3 native proof. A field match is only the first layer; native supply and the existing authority/checkout layers remain mandatory.

## 2. Only real products, seasonal behavior and Q5

Acceptance requires **both** the current loaded definition and the actual shop's advertised, priced, direct-stock contract. Catalogue presence alone is insufficient; a legacy identifier, reserved E4 code, Street Food economy row or a saved historical name alone is insufficient. Never synthesize a candidate, alternative identity, price, holder, recipe or stock to fill a gap. Loaded definitions still do not prove a nutrition/meal category merely by their names.

**Seasonal decision: refuse availability-gated products for E5.** A native member exists: `ItemHelper.IsAvailableInCurrentSeason(Item)` returns true for `SeasonName.None`; otherwise it requires `PlayerPrefSettings.SeasonalDecorations` and equality with `SeasonHelper.CurrentSeasonName`. [Batch M11–M12](e3-remaining-batch.md#shared-inspected-members-and-call-sites) establish the preference read and potentially synchronous cached/Addressable season loading. `Item.GetItemNameBySeason`, `BuildingRegistration.GetListOfItemsForSale` and layout paths additionally handle replacement/sale policy; the player menu getter simply returns its cached list. One helper would therefore not prove complete current-shop availability/substitution.

Apply the batch seasonal field refusal to **all** candidate products and participating holder/bag definitions: non-None/unknown season, special-gift/seasonal-sale/AI-seasonal flag or nonempty substitution array refuses. Also refuse a nonempty takeover replacement field rather than resolve it. Missing required metadata is unverified, not None. Do not call calendar/preferences/season loaders, choose replacements or infer Christmas from an ID. This excludes some genuinely available in-season goods; that is the intentional safe failure. Candycane/christmascookies might be nonretail or ungated; actual loaded fields decide, not their names. Supporting gated goods later needs a separate availability/invalidation proof.

Q5 still defers **drink-only meal substitution, bundles and carts**. The stock rule can naturally describe a simple finished drink or a single packaged bundle sold as one native retail unit, but it proves neither “this satisfies a meal” nor a multi-entry/resource bundle. No inspected universal food-versus-drink member was established: `ItemType` has retail/service flags, and `Item.isConsumable`, `saturation`, `energy` and consume animations are not a proven food/drink taxonomy. Do not invent `IsFood` or infer a class from a name or positive saturation. E5a reports the stock-contract match separately from meal admission. Multi-entry bundles, conversions and cart/vehicle commerce fail the contract naturally; a Street Food cart is not a `BuildingRegistration` full-service shop. No cart takeover or second checkout adapter is part of E5.

Under the **proposed Q1 default**, “ambiguous” means the resolved `MealClass` is `Unknown`: every non-original product starts there, regardless of a successful stock-contract match. The optional bounded config field in section 3 supplies an explicit class. E5b activates only `Food` entries that also pass every loaded-definition, stock, price, supply and whole-menu guard; `Drink`, `Bundle`, `NonFood` and `Unknown` remain outside meal admission. The original three retain their established `Food` classification. **With an empty override table E5b therefore activates no additional products** and hands back any shop advertising an unclassified new product; it delivers the dynamic eligibility/handoff mechanics, not automatic food classification. With, for example, an explicit `MealClass: Food` for an actually loaded vanilla or Street Food product, E5b can activate that product if the full native contract passes. No per-product code change is needed, and the config declaration alone proves no native eligibility. Q1 remains Peter's policy decision; implementers must not silently infer classes to make activation visible.

## 3. Neutral taste/meal fit and optional metadata

The existing seams are [FoodProducts](../../Core/FoodProducts.cs), [FoodMenuPolicy](../../Core/FoodMenuPolicy.cs), [MealRhythm](../../Core/MealRhythm.cs), [FoodChoicePolicy](../../Core/FoodChoicePolicy.cs), [FoodWeightedCommerce](../../Core/FoodWeightedCommerce.cs) and [B memory](../../Core/FoodMemoryPolicy.cs), with [A3 evidence](../checks/a3-weighted-choice.md) and [food-demand history](../food-demand.md). Preserve A2's ten saved bands and all original three product vectors: croissant `(0,350,1200)`, burger `(1200,100,0)`, hot dog `(900,0,450)` generalized-cost cents. No trait regeneration or hot-dog fallback.

For a new/modded product without taste/fit overrides, propose **taste band 2**, ratio **1**, and meal-fit vector **(900,100,450)**: the **per-meal medians of the original three penalties**, with unknown health/quality tags. These fit defaults apply only after explicit `Food` classification admits the product; they do not classify it. `TasteRatios[2] == 1` is multiplicatively neutral. The fit vector is a comparative policy baseline, not a claim of native nutrition or a zero-effect fit: at `FoodChoiceTasteWeight=1`, A3's fit denominators are **5.5 / 1.5 / 3.25** for breakfast/lunch/dinner. Zero would instead be the most favourable fit at every meal, giving an all-zero offer a 7× fit-factor advantage over breakfast burger or dinner croissant before A3's floor/temperature/cap; it is not the unknown-product default. Existing actual price, distance, queue, service-quality/district evidence and captured B contribution still act normally. Unknown tags add zero signal; no guessed nutrition, popularity, intrinsic quality, novelty, brand or loyalty bonus. E4's reserved 4–6 codes currently permit hashed taste projection; do **not** accidentally give new products that random preference simply by finding a wire code. Existing three tastes retain their saved bands. Any future optional individual projection must hash the canonical name with a versioned domain, saved seed and stable consumer ID, never a table index; it is not required for neutral E5.

The default combines neutral taste with a middle-ranked fit penalty per meal; it neither makes unknown goods universally best-fitting nor promises equal sales. Adding feasible offers can still change A3's competitive choice set and aggregate shop probability. Do not normalize/reweight the original three to compensate, add a menu-size boost or multiply B memory per product. B stays keyed by consumer/business generation and records actual experience once; cataloguing a new product is not a served visit or a new shop. With new items absent, original-choice weights/draws, memory and economy must replay unchanged. E5c must compare the default against each original product at each meal with other inputs equal, pin the denominators above and median ranking across supported taste weights, and test full-policy weights plus ordering and unused-catalogue growth independently. Keep legacy meal penalty zero and predecessor held-policy replay unchanged.

### Proposed config table, not a second loader

Recommend **config-only metadata for the first implementation**; no integration dependency on another mod. Add one optional top-level `FoodProductOverrides` table to `config/persistent-city.json` / `PrototypeSettings`, using the existing installed `<ModRootPath>/persistent-city.json` path. This is a deliberate narrowly bounded table extension to steps B–E's fixed flat product-key families, superseding those E5 families; other settings and loader stay unchanged. Example shape only, not a config edit:

```json
"FoodProductOverrides": {
  "foodtrucks:itemname_tacos": {
    "MealClass": "Food",
    "TasteBand": 2,
    "BreakfastFitCents": 900,
    "LunchFitCents": 100,
    "DinnerFitCents": 450
  }
}
```

| Value | Absent/default | Bounds and meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table | Empty | At most 256 unique ordinal canonical-name keys, each at most 256 UTF-16 code units, combined escaped JSON and UTF-8 at most 512 Ki each. Duplicates/invalid keys/overbound table reject this optional table with one bounded diagnostic; preserve unrelated config. Do not truncate it. |
| `MealClass` | `Unknown` for every new product; established `Food` for the original three | Exact enum strings `Unknown`, `Food`, `Drink`, `Bundle`, `NonFood`, at most seven ASCII characters. Under the proposed Q1 default only `Food` admits a meal; missing/invalid/wrong-type values resolve to `Unknown` with a bounded diagnostic for invalid input. This is authored meal policy, not a native food API or stock permission. Original-three classification is preserved; conflicting overrides are ignored and logged once. |
| `TasteBand` | 2 for new products; existing three keep saved individual traits | Integer 0–4, multiplicative ratio from A3's established bands; no per-consumer table. Initially apply this override only to new products. |
| `BreakfastFitCents`, `LunchFitCents`, `DinnerFitCents` | New products **900 / 100 / 450**, respectively; original three keep existing vectors | Integer 0–5,000 per meal; generalized-cost penalties, never money charged or a nutrition claim. An explicit 0 is the most favourable fit, not neutral. Legacy meal fit remains zero. |

Missing fields use the effective defaults. Follow existing numeric-string/endpoint-clamp and log-once conventions; out-of-range numeric values clamp, unusable/NaN/wrong-type or in-range fractional integers default. Reject unknown override fields/duplicate properties within an entry, using that entry's default (including new-product `MealClass=Unknown`) rather than accepting a typo as an instruction. Malformed whole-file JSON keeps the existing file-level failure path. Exact key lookup only: no case folding, trimming, aliases, suffix matching or fallback to vanilla names. Unloaded keys remain inert config metadata with a bounded diagnostic; they neither register nor enable a product. `Food` removes only the meal-class refusal once the definition is loaded; it cannot override native type, availability, price, resources, eligibility or capacity.

Load with the city unloaded, no watcher or per-frame parsing. Resolved metadata takes effect at the **next uncaptured, uncommitted context**, including later that day. Capture effective meal-class/taste/fit inputs for each offer in the single held decision (section 4); config changes after reload cannot rerank it. Committed trips/quotes/prices/receipts keep their terms and native revalidation. Class changes affect new menu admission and existing safe whole-business handoff, without rewriting captured terms. No daily config history or per-resident product array.

## 4. Canonical identity, saves, bounds and mod removal

Canonical identity is the **exact `Item.itemName` string**, e.g. `ba:itemname_salad` or `foodtrucks:itemname_tacos`. Use `StringComparer.Ordinal`, deterministic ordinal menu/descriptor ordering and length-delimited canonical-name draw inputs. A mod author reusing one name for a different product is outside PC's ability to infer semantic continuity; preserve the name's history and report the definition change.

| --- | --- |

### Missing mod is unavailable native evidence, not corrupt history

On reload or unregister, a previously saved name may no longer resolve. Valid saved names/history still load as opaque facts without requiring native definitions. Mark affected shops unserviceable immediately, withdraw new captures and hand back the **whole business** through existing ownership intervals/boundary. Do not crash market construction because `FoodProducts.Known` is false, clear the scope/history, regenerate consumers or remap to another item. Keep B memory attached to its original business generation; a missing product does not create a visit/penalty by itself.

An already-paid receipt stays paid; an uncertain intent is never retried merely because the definition vanished. Unpaid committed service can fail safely/refund according to the current exact-money state machine, retaining the resident's return journey and avoiding invented stockout blame for unknown evidence. Ownership cannot transfer until old work has drained. Structurally bad/future saves are a different case: preserve payload and stop unsupported execution under the early guard; do not use normal missing-mod handoff as permission to run overlapping sales engines.

If the same mod/name returns, resolve its current definition and revalidate the complete shop/holder contract before boundary reacquisition. The same saved historical name displays with its returning label; **retained** totals/references are available again, with no duplicate payments or regenerated taste. Ordinary fourteen-day history expiry still applies; preservation means no deletion on mod removal, not an unlimited archive. Native mod-removal cleanup may independently remove/refund cargo (Street Food's comment describes this); PC does not promise that reinstalling recreates native stock/equipment. Stock must really exist again. No native save-repair hook is introduced.

## 5. Invalidation and performance

No catalogue or holder scan, reflection, fingerprint polling or new allocation in the per-frame discovery path. Have it consume a prebuilt eligibility result, using constant-time revision/dirty checks. Existing necessary simulation work is not claimed to become allocation-free.

Proposed lifecycle hooks are **postfix/notification dirty marks**, not native writers or invented events:

The dirty hooks must be shown complete for native mutation paths before activation. Arbitrary third-party in-place field writes have no proven universal notification.
