## Reader API, refusals and isolation

On the Unity main thread, a bridge diagnostic may explicitly invoke `PersistentCity.Runtime.DonutFeasibilityReader.Read(existingRegistration)`. Obtain the registration from an already existing controller field, never `GetBuildingRegistration`. There is no new bridge command, automatic caller, scan of city shops, hook, update, timer or persistent data cache. Private authoring-tag field metadata binds once on the first explicit call; field reflection runs only inside a requested read, never per frame. It costs nothing when uncalled and has no per-frame work. Read at most 2,000 furniture entries, 128 menu/price entries and 128 cargo/producer/whitelist entries per furniture item. Return a detached snapshot; raw equipment names use a read-only wrapper. Do not retain a native registration across reload; explicitly select the new one.

`DirectStockFieldsVerified` means the selected registration meets the conservative **field shape**, not native compatibility approval. `Eligibility` always says Unknown, including agreement with native tag caches. `RawProductUnits` sums only matching direct candidate showcase cargo. `RawBagUnits`/`RawCounters` are business-wide unqualified observations, including unstaffed counters; there is intentionally no `Units`, `MealsPerHour`, capacity or available-meal field. No `min(product,bags)` claim from unknown staffing. `Price` is exact native binary32 money. Equipment names are copied from the observed loaded definitions, rather than guessed from constants. No recipe, price or catalogue entries are manufactured.

Refuse closed/nonplayer/unestablished shops, wrong simulator/entrance/bag policy, unloaded/missing product definitions, service products, missing/ambiguous advertised product or price, invalid/excessively precise prices, overbounds/missing/malformed collections, ambiguous furniture IDs, mismatched shelf whitelist, missing/nonconsuming resources, candidate workstations, alternate inputs, multiple outputs, nested/shared cargo, stock overflow, product/bag counter overlap, non-bag counter stock, or no direct shelf/counter. Zero stock/bags are known raw zero where the contract remains readable. An empty counter with no cargo is refused rather than inventing a bag definition. No atomic snapshot across concurrent mutation is promised: explicit main-thread invocation is required.

## Checkout seam decision: observation only

No new checkout seam is added. Existing `NativeFoodMarket.Buy` constructs `CustomerEntry`/`Order`/`OrderEntry` and temporarily approves a price via its owned Harmony prefix. The existing regression doubles simulate these writes; they cannot prove a new candidate without native asset data. Calling that path would require widening live support or manufacturing a diagnostic order, contrary to this slice's constraint. `RetailBusinessSimulator.ProcessCustomer` also creates random native customer data and makes other writes. Merely invoking it is not a safe read-only probe.

The rig must observe an independently created **natural native donut purchase** in a disposable, native-owned fixture, with bridge captures around it. No E3 code fabricates customers, nutrition, cargo, recipes, orders, prices or settlements. If no suitably stocked fixture exists without UI input, record NEEDS-INPUT; do not synthesize one through the reader. No clean candidate is certified by the assembly-only inspection: E3-donut stops before checkout/activation proof.

## Offline proof and E5 prerequisites

The [new standalone harness](../../tools/donut-feasibility-regression/Program.cs) compiles the actual reader against inspected native field shapes. Its explicitly named fixture shelf/counter definitions are conditional doubles, never claims about real asset settings. It checks direct/refused shapes, exact prices, zero stock/bags, equipment changes, converted and shared inputs/outputs, counter sharing, noninterference fingerprints and detached output. Throwing native getter/tag spies guard `ItemCached` and `HasTag`; source guards also ban `TagRef` properties, native setter reflection and method invocation. [Source checks](../../tools/donut-feasibility-regression/source_checks.py) ban gameplay callers/polling/hooks/native setters and pin the approved E3 shared reader/facades/project/harness and the landed donut comparison fixture. The shared read body/refusals and normalized original cases/project are compared to that landed contract after mechanical substitutions. Whole-tree preservation is a per-sync audit recorded in the owning checks file; the standing regression is independent of the moving development head. Its approved-file SHA-256 pins are embedded and normalize checkout CRLF to LF, so they also run after a squash merge in a clone without the original E3 commits. The initial merge-time audit compared all 209 preexisting Core/Runtime/Persistence/Compatibility/tests and catalogue/supply/checkout/save-regression blobs to its frozen base. Existing regressions are run separately, without edits. See [dated CHECKS](../checks/e3-donut.md) and [PR handoff](e3-donut-pr-description.md) for measured reader costs and the numbered 6 Nov checks.

E5-donut would first need loaded catalogue/equipment/requirements/staffing proof, an observed direct donut cargo resource (not plain donut/frosting or any conversion), bags/order/wholesale/retail/settlement and reload deltas including refill/shortage, natural checkout and combined native performance evidence. If the shipped configuration is converted-input only, stop this candidate as unsupported. E4's menu/identity/history/ownership-boundary work and separately reviewed product code/fit/taste/words remain prerequisites; none are implemented or inferred here. Current three-product invariants, uncertain-sale safety and saved contracts must stay intact after any future integration.
