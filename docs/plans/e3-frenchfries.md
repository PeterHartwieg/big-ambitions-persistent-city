## Conditional probe and preservation

[FrenchFriesFeasibilityReader](../../Runtime/FrenchFriesFeasibilityReader.cs) pins only `ba:itemname_frenchfries` through the existing private-constructor descriptor. Invoke explicitly on the Unity main thread with an existing registration, reselecting after reload. It adds no bridge command, hook, poll, timer, normal-play caller, city scan or saved/native-data cache. A single descriptor line is the only shared runtime edit; every read branch, refusal and snapshot field is unchanged. No generic holder abstraction is justified.

Conditional acceptance requires a loaded retail product, exact advertised ID/price, a `ShowcaseShelf` whose whitelist contains fries, consuming resources and a single nonnested nonnegative same-ID cargo entry, with no shared outputs; the detached equipment name comes from observed fields. A hypothetical showcase named industrial fryer can pass those fields; that test does **not** assert its shipped type. A non-showcase employee workstation advertising fries is refused with either fries or potato cargo. Storage stock is excluded. Existing 2,000-furniture / 128-entry bounds, malformed/shared/overflow/bag/counter refusals and zero-stock semantics remain intact.

`DirectStockFieldsVerified` establishes conservative raw field shape only. `Eligibility` stays Unknown. `RawProductUnits` is direct cargo; `RawBagUnits` and `RawCounters` are one unqualified business pool, including unstaffed counters, never capacity, reservations or available meals. Reads provide no concurrent-mutation atomicity guarantee. Unknown holder/type/whitelist/resource values keep this a **STOP**, not native feasibility approval.

The [own harness](../../tools/frenchfries-feasibility-regression/FrenchFriesFeasibilityRegression.csproj) links original doubles without changing the donut/cupcake/salad cases. It covers conditional success, mismatches, converted/shared inputs, named fryer workstation refusal, bags, missing/duplicate price, storage, detached snapshots and repeated/interleaved noninterference. [Source checks](../../tools/frenchfries-feasibility-regression/source_checks.py) pin full source and isolation. The existing guard removes only the new descriptor line before checking its **unchanged approved SHA-256 pins** and includes the new facade in its caller scan; salad's guard, original cases and facade files remain unchanged. Its older baseline reconstruction helper also strips exactly that descriptor line, retaining its original hashes and differential proof. ## What E5-frenchfries would need

First establish actual loaded retail/service type, holder type/whitelist/resources/outputs, authoring versus native tag-cache agreement, simulator, furniture/staff eligibility and exact price independently on a disposable native-owned fixture. If only workstation/converted/shared input or unreadable holder is observed, stop unsupported; do not reinterpret `itemsToProduce` or potatoes as finished stock.

Then prove natural checkout and settlement with independent direct stock, shared bag, refill, shortage/equipment, order/wholesale/revenue and save/reload/noninterference captures; combined mod-work p50/p95/max with p95 <5 ms and every spike >=30 ms flagged, whole-frame separate. E4's separately reviewed menu/identity/history/accounting-boundary framework, product code and product decisions are separate prerequisites. No E5 behavior is implemented here.
