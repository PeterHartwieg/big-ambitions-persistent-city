## Implemented

`StreetFoodVendorMarket` serves the original three products through E6b's public-contract bridge and coordinator. Logical preparation polls once per minute and copies at most 16 vehicles per continuation. Checkout reads the selected vehicle immediately before and after one `Sell`, verifies exact native day/cents and the selected one-unit decrement, checks that every other item's units are unchanged, and confirms the receipt. Only a definite API refusal with no sale and `uncertain=false` cancels the pending receipt. Exceptions and contradictory evidence leave it pending and block retry. Resident wallets continue through Core's existing confirmed-sale settlement; Street Food remains the player-revenue authority.

The Runtime NavMesh sampler refuses a stand without a finite point within 2 m. Menus with a price outside the resident money range never request ownership; an already-owned opening drains and hands back. Price changes, an old opening, a closed stand, owner/lease changes, an unsupported menu and insufficient service time refuse checkout before a receipt. Every vehicle scope and vehicle meal/decision/receipt is checked against the vendor table at market construction. Paid evidence and vendor identities survive reload with Street Food absent; vehicles then stay unserviceable.

`FoodMarkets` merges shop and vehicle reads, ranks saved positions before reading the nearest eight supplies (ordinal business-id tie-break), routes scope operations by channel, uses the later `EarliestMinute`, disposes both captures and both markets even if one disposal throws, and forwards rival calls to the shop market. `NativeFoodOwnership.ValidatePopulation` checks only Shop scopes against the manifest in both directions, using its unchanged identity matcher. A missing Shop scope or manifest entry still refuses construction. `NativeFoodMarket.cs` is unchanged.

The runtime constructs the composite, prepares vendors before logical advancement, preserves the lease through route retiming, compacts retired rows at an explicit save boundary with pending-receipt protection, and releases ownership at detach. `pc vendor status`, `pc vendor sales`, `pc vendor withdraw <vehicle>` and the vendor lines of `pc food decisions` are gated to FT cities. Withdrawal clears Desired immediately, waits for committed work, hands back, and allows the normal request for a later opening. A saved in-progress withdrawal stays withdrawn after reload. Decision diagnostics retain at most 50 detached traces. Every measured vendor work unit of at least 30 ms increments the status counter and logs its component and duration.

No schema, codec, wire, Core model, RLO, Street Food repository, body or non-original-product changes. Writer-30 golden fixtures are unchanged. No game, rig, ModsLocal or live bridge was used by this implementer.
