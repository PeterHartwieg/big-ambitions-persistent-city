# F6f — household resources and ordinary exchanges

Refs #93. Base `7a79f2a5` includes F6d `eef1bdc174`, F6e `7a79f2a5` and E6e `bd92a520`. Writer 32, its fields and its golden hashes stay unchanged. No pooling: F5 gives each person one wallet; resident housing shares stay as they are. No codec, wire, Model schema, F6g journal, fingerprint or meal-period producer changes.

## Implemented

- Paid outside visits use a bounded table: retail $5, leisure $12; `OrdinaryRetailVisitCents` and `OrdinaryLeisureVisitCents` clamp to 1–10,000 cents. These are modeled prices. The supported non-food types are gift, clothing, jewelry, florist and electronics shops, plus the existing gym/nightclub/cinema/theater leisure classes. Food and unsupported classes remain activity only. Parks and beaches need no ownership entry and stay free.
- Selection checks affordability before routing. Admission rechecks the person's spending limit, hold, arrears, account capacity and minute watermark. An unaffordable admission records no visit and pays nothing. The debit commits with the completed citizen event, after route continuations; pending-event saves and route rebuilds cannot charge a discarded event. Meal outings keep their food settlement and do not also pay C9.
- The sole counterpart is a modeled outside exchange: exact `Balance` → `Ledger.Discretionary`, plus the existing `VisitWatermark` (floor of admission minute; at most one debit per person per minute). Refusal changes neither cash nor dated totals. The extended cash identity holds after payment and at save.
- Runtime indexes the saved paid-destination IDs and existing native controllers once. Hourly F1 observations queue at most 16 place reads per simulation unit. All 16 reads finish before that unit publishes; unfinished refresh work holds the accounting clock before admission events. Published lookups are O(1). Missing, stale, unknown-owner, duplicate-address, player-owned and unsupported entries authorize activity only, with no debit. Reads use existing registration fields, including `RentedByPlayer` and `businessOwnerRivalId`; there is no native write.
- Ownership observations start at the next hour after attach/load. A transfer observed at that boundary invalidates a pending outside payment. A transfer between observations takes effect at the next boundary: the declared residual window is under one game hour and can create only labeled outside spending. Reload forgets ownership evidence and permits no outside debit until the next boundary. A route-index rebuild preserves the same published evidence.
- Visitors spend their existing opening endowment. With the F6f adapter installed, their next daily posting advances its watermark without daily origin income, housing, essentials or a home-food basket; reload cannot charge housing at its initial posting. No earlier payments are reclassified. F6e's separately seated city-commuter shift wages retain their existing attendance path; day visitors and carried outside employers receive no new city wage from F6f. The reserve for an endowment wallet is zero; holds and existing arrears still refuse purchases.
- The existing 14 dated rows retain exact retail/leisure totals. Small exact deltas avoid BigInteger allocation on admissions; save/output requests materialize the same wide values, with no wire change. The free projection exposes only first/last day, units and visit count. Ctrl+F8 builds the labeled city total line only on request. `pc ordinary status` / `OrdinaryExchangesDump()` expose managed totals and ownership refresh counters without a native refresh.
