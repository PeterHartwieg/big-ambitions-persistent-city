`Core/FoodEligibilityImage.cs` is the small shared immutable DTO seam: exact product name, exact `Money`, raw aggregate units, holder/definition/resource identities, one shared raw bag/counter pool, revisions and a bounded refusal enum. It has no meal-class, taste, wire-code, native object or saved field. `LoadedCatalogueEligibilityReader` is an explicit main-thread component with **no production caller or installed hook**. Its first continuation starts a bounded loaded-image rebuild; subsequent rebuilds require dirty marks. `TryGet` and a clean `Continue` only read cache state. Notifications coalesce, invalidate cached visibility immediately, discard interrupted work and publish complete images atomically. Catalogue changes queue the registered shops against the new global image; shop edits do not enumerate the catalogue. One continuation inspects at most 256 definitions or 128 furniture entries. Catalogue completion/overflow detection may require the next continuation; only one extra enumeration step detects entry 16,385, without reading it. Final publication sorts/copies at most 2,000 detached holder identities; that bounded finalization is included in continuation timings.

Catalogue names use exact ordinal text, 1–256 UTF-16 code units, without trimming, case folding, normalization or aliasing; whitespace-only, controls, invalid surrogates, backslash and pipe refuse. Null/unready, malformed/duplicate names, view/lookup disagreement, interrupted enumeration and overbound catalogues fail closed. Native collision resolution determines the loaded winner; PC never registers or repairs anything. Registered mod names, including `foodtrucks:itemname_dumplings` and `foodtrucks:itemname_choppedcheese`, receive exactly the same field checks as arbitrary vanilla IDs. The positive fixtures are hypothetical doubles, not Street Food equipment proof.

Every advertised entry must have a loaded non-service retail definition and exactly one explicit positive exact price. One refused entry refuses the complete menu, including E4's formerly missing-price subset case. The configured lower limit and eight-offer envelope are separate from the unchanged four-offer gameplay limit. Furniture, price/menu rows and cargo/whitelist/output/tag arrays retain E3's 2,000/128 bounds. Stock requires an observed consuming whitelisted showcase with one nonnested nonnegative same-ID cargo and empty or same-single-product outputs. Workstations, alternate/shared inputs, output-only declarations, ambiguous identities, reference-shared cargo, storage-as-supply, counter/product overlap, missing/malformed bags and overflows refuse. Raw zero stock/bags remains a known contract with zero raw availability. Participating product/holder/bag definitions refuse all non-None/unknown seasons, special-gift/sale/AI-seasonal flags, nonempty seasonal substitutions and takeover replacements; no seasonal/date/preferences helper runs.

The cache holds current-city registration references only as rebuild sources. `Reset`/`Dispose` clears queued/in-progress work and those references before unload; published images contain no native objects. E5b must own actual lifecycle notification installation and unload integration. Arbitrary third-party in-place field changes have no exhaustive proven notification surface. The current component therefore grants no service permission; E5b still needs current-definition/contract/price checks at capture/checkout, native effective-supply reconciliation and whole-business handoff. Cached raw quantities are never reservations or indefinitely frozen supply.
