## What is implemented

- **Walk, wait and transit legs are never leased.** `TryAcquirePhysicalJourney` now succeeds only for a journey on a traffic leg (a kernel vehicle with a link). The walk-to-traffic branch of `ObservePhysicalJourney` is gone; traffic starts from the logical due event. `TrafficCompleted` ends a lease with its traffic leg and queues the logical event, so the walk after parking runs on the logical clock. `TryHoldPhysicalDeparture` is removed. `PhysicalJourneyLease`, `TryAcquirePhysicalJourney`, `ObservePhysicalJourney`, `ReleasePhysicalJourney`, `IsPhysicalJourneyValid`, `TrySamplePhysicalJourney` and the kernel `SetPhysicalControl`/`ReportPhysicalProgress` callers stay for traffic legs until F2d.
- **Walker follow (`ObserveWalker`).** A walker binding holds its `CitizenState`, the committed `Journey` reference and its own route progress. Each frame it samples the logical pose (`SampleJourney`), advances its own progress with `WalkerFollow.Advance` (projection by `PhysicalProgress.TryProject` on its own leg, up to 16 corners, waits out a zero-distance leg with the logical clock, stops at the start of a car, bus or subway leg, capped at the logical progress) and takes the along-route lag (`WalkerFollow.RouteMetres`). Pace: 0 at or below 0.05 m (it stands at the logical pose and is never commanded past it), native pace up to 1.5 m, rising linearly to 1.5× native at 3 m. A changed journey reference or phase ends the follow.
- **Corrections.** Beyond 8 m, or when the logical leg is not a walk:
  - off screen: the body is moved to the logical pose (`Warp`, kind `Move`), or released if the person is no longer walking (kind `LagRelease`, with the mode handoff below);
  - on screen (Q3): it hurries at 1.5× for up to 10 s; then it fades at the next corner (its own leg changes, or it is within 0.3 m of the corner) or at a doorway (kind `Fade`, body released) and reappears at the logical pose through ordinary selection after the 2 s retry. A fading body that reaches no corner within a further 10 s is released (kind `LagRelease`). No body is moved while on screen.
- **Mode boundaries.** A walker that reaches the start of a car, bus or subway leg is released as a handoff (no correction), and its retry cooldown is cleared so a body of the new kind is acquired at the logical pose at the next selection. A car whose lease ends at traffic completion while the journey continues on foot is handed over the same way. Never a hold.
- **Arrival tail.** When this journey's arrival has been processed (the person is at the place, or waiting for its opening, at the route's end), the walker keeps walking to the door at catch-up pace for up to 10 s of real time. At the door, or after 10 s, it joins a vendor line, an optional stop or the door queue (the existing `ObserveArrival`), or goes in (released). Ending short of the door is recorded as `LagRelease`. This replaces `TryAwaitArrival`.
- **Departure tail.** When a new journey commits while a body is returning from an optional stop or a vendor stand, `OnOptionalDeparture` binds the follow to it at progress 0 and the body keeps walking back; nothing is held. At the entrance it follows with catch-up.
- **Telemetry (`WalkerMovementTelemetry`, Runtime memory only).** A bounded ring of the last 512 corrections with sequence number, frame, person ID, kind, body pose before and after (after = the logical pose it was moved to, or where a released body reappears) and the on-screen flag; counters for corrections, moves, on-screen moves, fades, lag releases, handoffs and faulted acquisitions; a lag histogram (0.05 m bins to 64 m, exact maximum) sampled once per frame per follower.
- **On screen** (`PhysicalPedestrianBody.OnScreen`): the body's renderer is enabled, `isVisible`, and its bounds intersect `Camera.main`'s frustum (planes computed once per frame). Unknown visibility (no camera or no renderer) counts as on screen.
- **Console.** `pc movement status` prints `followers`, `lag_samples`, `lag_p50_m`, `lag_p95_m`, `lag_max_m`, `corrections`, `moves`, `on_screen_moves`, `fades`, `lag_releases`, `handoffs`, `faulted_acquisitions`, `last_sequence` and the thresholds in force. `pc movement corrections since <seq>` prints `gap`, `oldest_sequence`, `last_sequence`, `count` and one line per entry after `<seq>`; `gap=true` when entries after `<seq>` were evicted. Both are read-only (`Runtime/MovementDiagnostics.cs`, routed from `Runtime/VendorDiagnostics.cs`).
- **Test-profile key `MovementFaultAcquirePercent`** (`PrototypeSettings`, 0–100, default 0): after selection and any traffic lease, this share of body acquisitions is refused (seeded `System.Random`), counted in `unavailable=` and `faulted_acquisitions`.
- **Harness.** `tools/movement-equivalence` models the follower for walkers (`PresentationDriver.ObserveWalker`; the double has no camera, so every correction is an off-screen move) and adds a walking-only fixture option (`MovementOptions.WalkingOnly`) and a `walking` arm family.

### Technical choices

- Thresholds (1.5 m, 8 m, 1.5×, 10 s hurry, 10 s fade bound, 10 s arrival tail, 0.05 m stop) are constants in `WalkerFollow` and printed by `pc movement status`; tune them from the rig's measurements.
- The body walks to the corner of its own leg (one `SetTarget` per leg, as before) and the pace is set each frame, so no path is computed per frame. The body is never targeted past its own leg's corner; at or ahead of the logical pose it stands.
- "Fade" is a release at the corner or doorway; native characters have no alpha fade. The person reappears through ordinary selection.
- A walker spawns at the logical pose (`PositionOf`) with its own progress set to the logical progress.
- Telemetry is static so sequence numbers keep increasing across a physical-population rebuild within a session.
