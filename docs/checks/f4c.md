## What is implemented

All in `tools/perf-gate/`:

- **`present.py`** (Decision 6): reads a PresentMon CSV and normalises its two schemas separately (upstream [v2.3.0 CSV columns](https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon/blob/v2.3.0/README-ConsoleApplication.md#csv-columns)). In 1.x the row time is the present (`TimeInSeconds`, or QPC ticks), `Dropped` marks a present that was not shown, and `msBetweenDisplayChange` is the displayed interval ending at that row. In 2.x the row time is the start of the frame's CPU work: `CPUStartQPC` (ticks, needs the QPC frequency), `CPUStartQPCTime` (ms) or `CPUStartTime` (ms since recording started). The frame closes at `CPUStart + FrameTime`, and that close is the time joined to the probe frame's end. `DisplayedTime` says how long the frame stayed on screen, `NA` if it was not shown, so the interval ending at a frame's display is the previous shown frame's `DisplayedTime`. When `DisplayLatency` shows that a row between the two displays is missing, the interval comes from the two display times instead. `CPUStartDateTime` alone is refused. GPU busy is `GPUBusy` (1.x `msGPUActive`), CPU busy `CPUBusy`, CPU wait `CPUWait`. A missing column gives `None`, reported as `unknown`. A trace with no display timing yields no presented series, never CPU or present gaps in its place, and the window's join is not established. Optional filters: process ID, application. A frame is called `gpu`-bound when GPU busy is ≥ 80 % of its presented interval, otherwise `cpu`-bound. Without GPU busy it is `unknown`.
- **QPC join** (`present.estimate_offset`, `present.join`): the probe's frame rows carry the frame-start QPC and the whole-frame interval (F4b's `qpc`, `whole_frame_ms` and `status.stopwatch_frequency`). Candidate clock offsets pair the longest probe frames with present-to-present gaps of similar length. Only intervals ≥ 2 × the median frame count as anchors. A candidate must place at least 3 of those long gaps, and at least half of them, on frames of similar length. The offset is then refined in quarter-frame steps (±3 frames) on the first 10,000 frames by the agreement of joined gaps with frame intervals. The supplied offset is tried first and kept unless a shift lowers the mean disagreement by at least max(0.05 ms, 20 %), so a flat cadence or a flat prefix before the anchors leaves it in place. When anchors set the offset, the refined offset must still place as many long intervals on frames of similar length, within half a median frame; otherwise the anchored offset is kept. A one-frame misalignment still joins almost every frame, so the join count alone cannot decide the offset. A QPC trace without enough long anchors uses 0 as the hint and is still refined. A seconds trace without anchors is refused. Each frame then takes the present nearest its end, within half a frame, at most one each way. A present missing from the trace leaves its frame unjoined. A dropped present stays joined, has no presented interval, and the next displayed interval spans the gap.
- **`attribute.py`** (Decision 7, P1–P4 inputs): `python tools/perf-gate/attribute.py <export.json> [--trace <csv>] [--trace-for <window>=<csv>]... [--qpc-frequency N] [--process-id P] [--player-log <Player.log>]... --export-out <enriched.json> --json-out <attribution.json> --md-out <attribution.md>`. It writes a copy of the export and leaves the input unchanged. Each joined window gets:
  - `presented_ms`: displayed intervals whose covered frames are all clean (not warm-up, not diagnostic), the series F4a's P1, P2 and P4 read. An interval that spans a diagnostic or warm-up frame through a dropped present is left out;
  - `stall_classes`: the classes of each presented interval ≥ 50 ms, keyed by its index, which F4a's P3 reads;
  - `whole_frame_stall_classes`: classes of each clean probe frame with whole-frame ≥ 50 ms, keyed by frame number;
  - `presentation`: offset and its source, clock, joined / clean frames, trace coverage, intervals, GPU busy and CPU wait `reported` or `unknown`, bound counts, `aligned` (trace covers the window and ≥ 99 % of clean frames joined, G3), and `established` (aligned and the trace has display timing);
  - `receipts.presentation_trace`: filled `pass`/`fail` from `established` when the rig left it empty. A rig `pass` becomes `fail` for every window whose supplied trace was not joined, whatever stopped it: frames without QPC times, no clock offset, no display timing, poor coverage. A rig `fail` is never raised to `pass`.

  The attribution report lists every stall with its classes and evidence, every unattributed stall as open evidence (window, kind, frame, interval, game minute, condition), the M2 frames with their advancement fields, and RLO lines that matched no unique frame.
- **RLO frame-diagnostic lines**: the parser reads RLO's `Session: a frame of N ms; ...; where it went: <phase> N ms, ...; the processor: ...; the collector: ...; the game: ...` line, in the format of the 9 November excerpt in PR #170's `wholeframe-traces.json`. Phase names were checked read-only against the installed RLO DLL's strings. A line comes from `rlo_lines` in an export window (string or `{line, frame | first_frame, last_frame}`) or from `--player-log`. The line has no frame number, so it attaches to a frame given explicitly or to the one probe frame whose whole-frame time is within max(1.5 ms, 2 %) of `N`, inside its window or frame range. Every probe frame, clean or not, counts as a rival, and a line matching two frames attaches to neither. A `--player-log` line has no window or range and may describe a frame outside every window, so it is context only and is listed under `rlo_unmatched`.
- **F4a hook** (`analyze.py`, 3 lines in `stalls_of`): the analyzer's stall table reads `whole_frame_stall_classes` for whole-frame stalls instead of always printing `unclassified (F4c)`. P1–P4 needed no analyzer change, because F4a already reads `presented_ms` and `stall_classes`.
- **`fixtures/present/`**: `synthetic_trace.py` (probe frames plus a PresentMon 1.x/2.x CSV with offset, missing and dropped presents, stalls, union and GPU/CPU fields, and the 7-hour timing session), `presentmon2-sample.csv`, `presentmon1-sample.csv`, `rlo-frame-lines.txt` (the 9 November excerpt, a LateUpdate/UpdateCanvasRectTransform frame, a loading/saving/scene frame with a collection, a present-wait frame, and a line that is not a frame diagnostic).

### Classifier rules

Excess = interval − baseline. The baseline is the p50 of the reference window F4a would pair with the candidate: same condition, script, arm and repetition, valid by F4a's rules (receipts, short, aborted, first window), and matched on checkpoint minute, game-time span and pace (`analyze.matched`). The p50 is the presented p50 for presented stalls and the clean whole-frame p50 for whole-frame stalls. Without such a reference a candidate has no baseline and no `reference-rate`. Its excess is then the whole interval, which is enough to show `pc-union` against any p50. A union that would meet the share only against the window's own p50 is recorded as `pc_union: unresolved`. A reference window's baseline is its own p50. `need` = 50 % of the excess. A presented interval covers every probe frame since the previous displayed present. A whole-frame stall uses the present its frame joined to as trace evidence, the same evidence the presented table uses, whenever the join is `aligned` (trace covers the window and ≥ 99 % of clean frames joined). That holds even when the trace has no display timing and `established` is false.

| Class | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `pc-union` | PC union mod-work summed over the covered frames ≥ `need` and > 0. Evidence: union ms, frame, largest component and component times, plus advancement fields (below). |
| `gc` | Probe GC count > 0 in a covered frame. RLO's collector text is not used. With no probe GC field the stall records `gc: unknown`. |
| `save-load-scene` | Probe `saves` > 0, `scene` or `loading`; or the attached RLO line says `saving` or names a scene loaded or unloaded. |
| `native-frame` | An attached RLO line names a phase (not `the rest`, `outside the engine's steps`, `waiting for the last frame to be shown` or `PresentAfterDraw`) whose time minus the PC union is ≥ `need`; or the trace's CPU busy minus the union is ≥ `need`. |
| `gpu-present` | Trace GPU busy ≥ `need`, or trace CPU wait (present queueing) ≥ `need`. |
| `reference-rate` | The matched reference has stalls within a size factor of 1.5 at ≥ half the candidate's rate per 5 minutes in that size band. |
| `unattributed` | None of the above. |

Whole-frame or presented time minus the PC union is never PC's (`not_pc_ms` in each record), and PC union time is subtracted before any phase or CPU-busy time counts as native. Reference windows get the same classifier. Their union is 0, so they never carry `pc-union`, and they have no `reference-rate`.

**Advancement fields (fix-128).** For each frame in a stall or in the M2 list: when the `AdvancePopulation` sub-scope is bound and the frame has no advancement time, the frame gets **no** advancement fields. A 0 in F4b's `components_ms` array is a scope that did not run, not an advancement call, unless a per-call record exists for the frame. Otherwise it gets the advancement time (component, or F4b's per-call record), the per-call GC delta and optional-stop ms (or `unknown`), and the summary's slowest-unit fields. Those fields come from the summary only for the one clean frame of the window whose advancement time is within 0.1 ms of the observation's maximum. Candidates are all clean frames of the window, not only spikes, so a maximum two frames could match attaches nowhere. Every other advancement frame shows `unknown`.

## Known limits

- F4b's probe export (`qpc`, `components_ms`, `status.stopwatch_frequency`, `saves`, `scene`, `loading`) and the RIG-PRESENT CSV are read as documented at this base. Neither has been produced natively yet.
- RLO lines carry no frame number or QPC time. Duration matching attaches a line only when exactly one frame in its window or range matches. Bare Player.log lines are context only; to attach them, the rig has to put them in the window's `rlo_lines`.
- `native-frame` from the trace uses PresentMon's `CPUBusy` (app-side wall time up to Present), not per-thread CPU time.
- Diagnostic and warm-up frames are excluded from `presented_ms` and from stalls, as F4a excludes them from frame statistics.
- S4's stall-time alignment with lag samples is not part of this row and is unchanged.
