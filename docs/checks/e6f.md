## Implemented

Vehicles admit every name E5b4's held product policy executes: an original product, or a canonical name in the published loaded-name image that the resolver (config over API over defaults) classes as Food. Dumplings, chopped cheese, vanilla and Bakery names therefore enter vehicle menus, decisions, tickets and checkout with E5c name identity, E5d food-by-default metadata and E5e seeded taste, all through E5b4's existing capture. Admission stays whole-vehicle: one Drink, Bundle, NonFood, Unknown, unloaded or non-canonical name keeps the entire vehicle with Street Food, with the reason `menu item not executable: <name>`.

- `Runtime/StreetFoodVendorMarket.cs`: an optional `productPolicy` getter. The market reads it once per logical minute in `Prepare`, before the revision poll, and passes `IFoodProductPolicy.Executable` to `VendorCoordinator` (request, acquire, `CanServe`) and to the checkout recheck (`LiveOpening`) in place of `VendorRules.OriginalThree`. Without a getter, admission stays the original three. With a getter, the market holds admission until the first ready policy (`Coordinator.Deferred`): the coordinator applies nothing, and the saved ownership and canonical menus stay served, so committed residents finish their held decisions. When a ready policy later goes away during a catalogue rebuild, the market keeps the last ready policy. Both waits, the hold after load and the rebuild, are bounded by `PolicyPreparationMinutes` (15 logical minutes). If the policy is still not ready after that, the market fails closed to the original three and hands dynamic vehicles back after drain.
- `Core/VendorStands.cs`: a vehicle with more items than the resident choice menu width (`FoodMenuMaximumOffersPerShop`) stays with Street Food with the reason `menu has more than <N> items for resident choice`, because Core never offers a resident a wider menu.
- `Core/VendorStands.cs`: `VendorCoordinator.Revalidate()`. A new policy object (config, API publication or loaded-name image changed) applies the current Street Food image again at the next unit, without waiting for a new Street Food revision. A vehicle whose menu became non-executable stops new captures at once and is handed back after its committed residents drain; a vehicle whose menu became executable is requested and served from a later opening under the existing rules. E5b4's fail-closed fallback to the original three hands a dynamic vehicle back the same way.
- `Runtime/PersistentCityMod.cs`: the existing `FoodProductPolicySource` is built before the vehicle market and passed to it, together with the menu width. The composite (`FoodMarkets.ProductPolicy`), shop admission (`NativeFoodMarket.ProductPolicySource`, from E5b5) and vehicle admission read one policy.

Held decisions replay as saved. A held vehicle decision keeps its revision-2 tuples across reload, also when config or the API then change; if the change makes the vehicle non-executable, the committed resident is not served (no `Sell`, no receipt) and keeps their money. With Street Food absent the city loads with its vehicle scope, market history and receipts unchanged and serves no vehicle; when Street Food returns, each vehicle is admitted again only if its menu is executable under the current policy, at a later opening.

No schema, codec, wire or model change; writer 36 and its golden hash are unchanged. `Persistence/*`, `Core/PhysicalJourneys.cs`, `Runtime/PhysicalPopulation.cs`, `Runtime/NativeFoodMarket.cs`, `Runtime/NativeFoodCandidates.cs`, `Core/FoodMenuPolicy.cs`, `Core/FoodChoiceSettings.cs` and `Core/VendorState.cs` are untouched. No game, rig, ModsLocal or bridge was used.

Existing vendor tests that pin the original three (`StreetFoodVendorTests`, `VehicleMarketTests`, the acceptance `CityScenario`) are unchanged: they build the coordinator or market without a policy, which keeps the original-three predicate.
