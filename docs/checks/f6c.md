## What F6c adds

- **Week over week per managed shop** (`Core/FoodFeedback.cs`, `FoodFeedback.Weeks`). This week is days today − 6 … today (today in progress); last week is today − 13 … today − 7, so both windows sit inside the existing 14-day `FoodShopDay` rows. No new history. It shows purchases, sales and the change. Rows outside both windows, from other shops or from later days are ignored, so the result is the same whether or not the oldest row has been pruned yet.
- **Evaluated offers by cause, as week totals of the daily rows:** chosen, unaffordable, travel/time, closed or too late, stock unavailable, no staffed counter, service/queue, preferred another option, unknown evidence. Example line: `No staffed counter: 41 evaluated offers this week (last week 3)`. Every block carries `FoodFeedback.OfferMeaning`: these are offers, a resident who decides again counts again, and they are not located by hour or meal. Nothing is attributed to an hour, meal or resident.
- **Coverage is tracked independently of shop activity; unknown days are labeled, never shown as zero.** `FoodFeedback.RecordingSince` is the day from which the city's food history was certainly recorded: the earlier of the saved `ChoiceStartsDay` (always set to the food city's creation or migration day + 1 in writer 25) and the earliest kept row of any shop. A shop's coverage starts at the earlier of that day and its own first kept row, so a covered day without a row (no evaluated offers or purchases) counts as zero and a quiet first day of last week no longer suppresses the change. Days before coverage are genuinely unknown: a window that starts before it is labeled `no record before day X`, a window that ends before it says `no record last week`, and the panel says `No change shown: last week has days with no record (no evaluated offers, or before recording began).` No per-shop management start is saved, so a shop that became managed after city recording began shows its earlier covered days as zero evaluated offers recorded at that shop.
- **Rival label:** the D3a outflow line is prefixed with `FoodFeedback.RivalEstimateLabel` = `"Rival availability: estimated"`. This one constant is F6b's one-line wording change. `Core/RivalFood.cs`, `RivalOutflowHistory` and rival settings are not edited.
- **City money summary on the current per-consumer budgets.** The four existing lifetime-total lines are unchanged, plus one line of current budget states: how many consumer budgets are behind on bills, have no money to spare for prepared food (spending limit zero without a purchase hold), and went without the home-food basket at their last daily posting. The line says that each represented consumer has its own budget and that these are not households.
- **Incremental aggregates replace the O(consumers) panel scans.** `FoodMoneyCounters` keeps exact 128-bit totals of nine budget amounts, four state counts, the uncertain-purchase count and the blocked-return count. Attached `ResidentBudget`, `ResidentMeal` (`Status`, `Budget`, `Excursion`) and `MealExcursion.Unresolved` setters report each change: O(1), no allocation. Unattached objects (codecs, tests, copies) pay one null check. The counters are transient: `FoodCommerce` rebuilds them with one scan whenever a market attaches (load, migration, route rebuild). Enrolment adds new consumers. A count mismatch (a consumer appended without the owner) triggers one rescan. `FoodMoneyCounters.Rescan` is the reference scan with the same definitions. The panel also groups history, places and native statements once per build instead of searching them per shop.
- **Inspector words for current budget states** (`ResidentBudgetWords`, shown in the popup after memory words, never numbers): "behind on bills", "no money to spare for eating out right now", "could not afford groceries at home on the last bill day", "living on support income". They use the same state test as the panel counts.
- **Rig diagnostic** `PopulationRuntime.FoodFeedbackOverview` (built only when read): counters against a rescan (`countersMatchRescan`), request/rebuild counts read before its own snapshot, the money text, the rival label and each managed shop's week text.
- **Unchanged:** behaviour, economy, every decision input, saves (writer 25, no new field; counters are never serialized), native reads and writes, RLO, the 3 s panel cadence, and the rule that text is built only while the panel is open.

Not in F6c (later slices, per the plan): the count of own-shop customers who chose the player because a rival was full (needs F6b), income earned versus scheduled and household strain (F6e/F6f), meal-period causes and the change journal (F6d/F6g).
