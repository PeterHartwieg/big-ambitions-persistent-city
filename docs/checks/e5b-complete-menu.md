### Refusal boundary and remaining implementation

**The failing contract is timely and exhaustive mutation invalidation plus effective-holder reconciliation before acquiring/suppressing a complete dynamic business.** E5a's 19 discovered methods are signatures, not an installed or exhaustive notification surface. This task does not assert otherwise. Read-only inspection confirms `ItemHelper.SubtractFromStock` directly changes cargo amount, can synchronously refill and takes different callback branches; `ReStockingHelper` also moves amounts directly; `CargoInstance.ParseIntoItemInstance` can replace cargo lists; business menu refresh writes the menu/providers/prices. Public native fields also permit unobserved in-place writes. A postfix list alone is insufficient evidence.

Consequently **dynamic `Supported`/candidate acquisition, lifecycle hook installation, current-definition/holder comparison, effective native-producer reconciliation, dynamic dirty recovery and schema-25 held-policy execution remain incomplete**. The historical original-three live product guard and four-offer limit remain. There is no timer, fingerprint scan, new allow-list or switch that grants new native product authority. No cart/truck commerce, bundle execution, API, new schema or product codes were added. Conditional positive doubles below do not cover this missing integration.

The initial implementation held catalogue/supply refusals until reload; review round 1 below replaces that session restriction for the historical original-product contract. A refusal immediately withdraws service and protects ending work through the existing native handoff. Once that interval has closed, an advancing accounting-hour ownership refresh performs a fresh complete-menu/price, authored-bag and native supply read. Only success removes the mark and requests normal future acquisition; same-hour refreshes, failed reads and live native customers cannot resume service. A transient known zero stock/bags/staff does not request this handoff. Missing or unreadable cargo stays unverified, never invented zero. Registration replacement retains the existing generation/drain rules. Unknown definition/holder changes are not relabeled as a confirmed stockout.
