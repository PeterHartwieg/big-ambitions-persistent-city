## Implemented contract and limits

- `StepBudget` posts at most **32** consumers per unit, ordered by due minute and ordinal consumer identity. Citizen/traffic events at that minute retain their earlier order; every posting still precedes meals at that minute. Each budget's existing next-posting timestamp and exact ledger reconstruct the transient queue after reload. No saved cursor or schema change.
- The derived posting heap caches ordinal ranks and due times during construction/finance enrollment. Posting repairs its existing slot instead of allocating a sorted-set node. No per-frame initialization, reflection, polling, new scans or diagnostic strings.
- Every frame replay after a route-input replacement remains sliced. No restart count grants an infinite budget. A finite native route continuation admits at most **8 stages**, time-aware path-stage admission (recent observed query tail reserved against remaining budget), and a **16-path safety ceiling per planner/frame** across repeated calls. Road A* admits one **64-expansion** slice per finite road continuation. Explicit non-frame `AdvanceTo`/`Plan` calls retain synchronous semantics.
- Genuine route-input churn keeps the unstarted event and logical barrier; later events cannot pass it. Mod-owned refreshes are deferred for one event so progress continues during managed refresh churn; actual native invalidation still restarts safely. F3 does not invent a route, discard demand, or impose F1's overload/backpressure policy. The 120-restart test verifies bounded attempts and byte-identical preserved state; the sustained managed-refresh tests verify completion and equivalence without stopping replacements.
- Native campaign/UI inputs are captured coherently at the actual boundary for up to **1,024 actual lifecycle read candidates**. Retained historical scopes do not consume admission. Detached immutable observations then publish in **at most 16 business/image work units per continuation**, with FIFO hourly order and atomic final publication. Logical events, including pending route commitment, wait for this preparation; explicit save completes all captured work before serializing. No native object survives into a continuation, no supported hour is omitted, and no schema/cursor is added. The native capture is non-preemptible and has its own measured tail, separate from resumable managed processing.

Advance planning/versioned-input investigation and the indivisible-unit inventory are in the [performance record](../perf/pc-f3-bounded-bulk-work.md). Removing the global route barrier safely requires captured event inputs and deterministic commit validation; F3 documents why it does not reorder events or introduce speculative native work.
