## Wire

`"Vendors":[1,[row, ...]]`, rows in ordinal vehicle-id order (the table's own order).

- Live (0) or retired (1): `[state, vehicle id, kind, generation, place id, stand | null, deferred stand | null, authority]`.
- Tombstone (2): `[2, vehicle id, generation, place id, business id]`.
- Stand: `[opening id, x, y, z, opened day, opened minute, closes day, closes minute, published]`.
- Authority: `[desired, requested hour, from hour, until hour, suspended, opening id, handback pending]`. The plan lists the first five. Opening id and handback pending are also part of E6b's `VendorAuthority`, and without them a reload would re-acquire a served opening and lose a pending handback, so they are saved too.

The place id and business id are derived from the vehicle id. The reader requires the saved strings to equal the derivation. Identities keep the plan's form, `vendor:<escaped id>` and `streetfood:vehicle:<escaped id>:generation:<g>`. A 128-unit id escapes to at most 1,152 characters (nine per BMP unit), so a place id is at most 1,159 characters and a business id at most 1,193. Every saved field that can hold one allows that maximum for a vendor identity only, and keeps its own 256 or 512 bound for anything else. These fields are the table, scopes, history, meals, meal outcome records, decisions and their shops, awareness candidates, person place references, optional-stop places and business references, plus the streaming string check from the vendor schema. The same bound (`VendorNames.Bound`) applies when the simulator builds its awareness catalogue. `PlacePublished` is not saved: a live or retired row has its place exactly while it has a stand, and the writer refuses a table that breaks this.

Business-reference place cell: a city place keeps its three base-36 digits of the ordinal `Places` index. A vendor row's place is written as `v` plus two base-36 digits of the row's position in the saved table, counting live, retired and tombstone rows (at most 1,152; two digits reach 1,295). `WireContext.FromSource` decodes the table before business references are decoded. `Check` accepts the `v` form only in the vendor schema and below 1,152. `ReadJson` resolves `v` only through the table, and refuses a city index that names a vendor place.

The same `v` form is used by every compact cell that can name the place where a person is: a resident's recent visit, a visitor's routine place, destination and venues, and a visitor's last arrival. Resident place and destination are plain strings already. Person validation resolves the live and retired vendor places the simulator rebuilds, with the stand's position and hours. A recent visit keeps its row retired, so the place still exists. Compaction waits until the visit ages out of the 32 kept visits.

## Reader and writer rules

- **Bounds before allocation.** Live ≤ 64, live + retired ≤ 128, tombstones ≤ 1,024. These are counted as the rows stream, before each row is built.
- **Rows.** Vehicle ids are 1 to 128 UTF-16 units with no control characters or unpaired surrogates, strictly ordinal (so duplicates are refused). Kind is `cart` or `truck`, and generation is at least 1. A stand has an opening id of at least 1, finite coordinates, an opening minute from 0 to 1439, and either no close (-1/-1) or a close after the opening. A deferred stand needs a stand. Hours are from -1 to the maximum minute / 60. A tombstone keeps only its identity: the writer refuses a tombstone with a kind, stand or non-default authority, because the wire could not carry it. Integers must be canonical and strings exact.
- **Restore.** Tombstones are admitted first, then retired rows, then live rows, so `VendorTable`'s own transitions never pass through a state that breaks the capacity invariant.
- **Food places.** `FoodPlaceLookup` accepts the place of a live or retired row with a stand, at the stand's position, for scopes, history rows, meals, decision shops and excursions. Tombstone places are not food places.
- **Scopes by channel.** A Shop scope must name a city place, never a vendor place. A Vehicle scope must name a live or retired row with a stand and carry that row's current business id. Every such row must have a Vehicle scope. The writer also refuses a vendor table or Vehicle scope in a frozen older writer, so an older writer never drops vendor state silently.
- **Business references.** The place of any row, tombstones included, is accepted only for the row's signature with a canonical generation from 1 to the row's current generation (`streetfood:vehicle:<escaped id>:generation:<g>`). Any other business id at a vendor place is refused. A city place may not use the `vendor:` prefix.
- **Records.** Both record counters charge one record per row.

Cases in `tests/VendorSaveTests.cs`, all through the real `SaveCodec.Write` and `SaveCodec.Read`:

- **Next-free writer.** `VendorSchema == ResearchSchema + 1 == CurrentSchema`, and `LatestDevelopmentSchema` is writer 29. The frozen writer 29 refuses a city with vendor rows or a Vehicle scope.
- **Writer 29's shape kept.** On three vendor-free cities, writer 30 minus `"Channel":0,`, minus `,"Vendors":[1,[]]` and with the schema label swapped equals writer 29 byte for byte. Every existing scope writes Channel 0.
- **Round trip.** Live, retired and tombstone rows, a deferred stand, suspended and handback flags: the payload round-trips byte for byte and field for field. Vendor places are not in `Places`. The loaded simulator has each live or retired row's place at its stand, with one opening window when published and none when not. A route rebuild keeps the same place objects.
- **Bounds.** 64 live save, 65 are refused. 64 live + 64 retired save, 129 are refused (also 129 retired alone). 1,024 tombstones save, 1,025 are refused. The full 1,152-row table with one reference per row writes `v00` to `v` + `MemoryWire.Number(1151, 2)` in order and reads every place back.
- **63 + 2 retired.** 63 vehicles open, get history rows and are sold (63 retired). Two more open and get history, the city reloads, both are sold, which leaves 65 retired rows. Compaction removes nothing because history still names them, and the city saves and reloads.
- **Admission at 128.** 64 sold (retired, with history) and 64 new live; one more sold and a new one bought gives 63 + 65, and the newcomer is refused with "vendor table full: 128 live and retired vehicles". The city saves and reloads.
- **Memory slot across tombstone and return.** A vehicle opens, a served experience is recorded (business reference plus memory entry), and the city reloads. The vehicle is sold, and it is not compacted while history names it. The city reloads again. History expires, the row becomes a tombstone (`[2,"A",1,"vendor:A","streetfood:vehicle:A:generation:1"]`), and the city reloads with the slot intact. The vehicle returns as generation 2 and is served, and a new experience is recorded. After a reload both references are intact and both use `v00`. A generation-3 reference is refused by the writer and, forged into the payload, by the reader.
- **Return without a new experience.** Generation 2 returns, a pending meal holds its stand across a reload, and the vehicle is sold. Compaction waits for the meal. Once the meal clears, the row becomes a generation-2 tombstone, not removed, because the generation-1 reference remains. It saves and reloads with that slot intact.
- **`v` form.** City place `001`, then `v00`, `v01`, `v02`. `v03` in a three-row table, `vZZ` (beyond 1,152), a lowercase digit, `v0` and `v000` are refused. `v00` with another row's business is refused. A city index naming a forged `vendor:zz` city place is refused by the reader, and the writer refuses that city place. A `v` cell in a frozen writer-29 payload is refused by the writer-29 reader.
- **Other business ids at a vendor place.** Refused by the writer and the reader: another signature, generation 0, `01`, `+1`, no generation, a plain id.
- **Ids.** 128-unit ids round-trip, 129 are refused. A non-ASCII id with a space and an emoji escapes and round-trips. A control character is refused.
- **Maximum-length escaped ids (reviews Sol-r1-1, Sol-r2-1).** Ids of 128 `中` (the 1,159 and 1,193 maxima exactly), 128 spaces, 64 surrogate pairs and 128 `é` keep their escaped identities. Each round-trips with a Vehicle scope, a meal and its outcome record at the place, a resident eating there, a business reference and a remembered tombstone. A 513-character ordinary business id is still refused. A city with a 28-unit (259-character place) and a 128-unit `中` id saves, reloads and advances two days through awareness rebuilds, then saves again; without the catalogue bound both cases fail with "Invalid awareness alternative" (review Sol-r3-1).
- **Persons at stands (review Opus-r1-1).** A resident eating at a stand, one walking to it, and one with a retired stand among their recent visits (written `v01`) each save and reload. A visitor at a stand writes `v00` in its routine and in its arrival, and reloads. A visit `v` index outside the table is refused, and the frozen writer 29 refuses a visit at a vendor place. A recent visit keeps a sold vehicle's row retired across a reload, and the row is removed once the visit is gone.
- **Duplicates and malformed rows.** Duplicate and unordered ids are refused. So are a place or business that differs from the derivation, generation 0 or `01`, state 3, a surplus slot, kind `van`, an until hour of -2 and a missing authority slot.
- **Scope checks.** A Vehicle scope without its row, a row with a stand but no scope, a Shop scope at a vendor place, a Vehicle scope at a city place, a Vehicle scope with another generation, and channel code 2. A meal at a missing vendor place or at a tombstone's place, and a history row at a missing vendor place. A deferred stand without a stand ("stand without vendor place"), a stand whose place is not published and the reverse, a stand closing before it opens. A tombstone with a kind or authority. A null, wrong-version, surplus-slot or missing table.
- **Deferred stand move.** A pending meal holds stop 1 while the vehicle opens at stop 2. The move is deferred, and the city reloads. After reload the row still has the deferred stand, and the rebuilt place is at the old position with no hours. When the meal clears, the place moves to stop 2, opens and is served. The city reloads again with no deferred stand.
- **Churn past 64.** Six rounds of 40 vehicles: each round opens, saves, sells, saves, compacts, saves. Unremembered rows are removed and remembered ones become tombstones, 24 after 240 vehicles. Every save loads.
- **Older and future cities.** `tests/Writer30/refused-writer-29-minimal.json` holds frozen writer-29 bytes. It is pinned by SHA-256 and equal to writer 29's pinned `current-schema-minimal`. Codec, store, session and the capability probe refuse it with `DevelopmentSchema` and "start a new city". Store and session writes of a replacement city fail, every key stays byte-identical, and the frozen writer-29 reader still loads it. A future label is refused as Future and kept the same way.
