Plan: [C5 section 4, row C5c](../plans/c5-agency-research.md#c5c--save-schema), with sections 5, 6, 7 and 9. ## Scope

- **Implemented.** `CityState.Research` (`Core/Model.cs`) holds the C5a `ResearchLedger`. A fresh city starts with an empty ledger. `CityState.CurrentSchema` is `PersonTable.WorkplaceCapacitySchema + 1`, the next writer after F5e, which is 29 at this base. `Persistence/ResearchWire29.cs` holds the section codec, its bounds and `SaveCodec.ResearchSchema`. `Persistence/SaveCodec.cs` wires the converter, the streaming check, the validation, the frozen `ReadWriter28` reader and a writer-28 contract resolver that omits the section. `Core/MarketResearchFriends.cs` grants `PersistentCity.Persistence` access to Core internals, so the codec can read sealed cells and call `ResearchLedger.TryRestore`. Runtime still reaches the numbers only through `TryRead`.
- **One line outside the owned files.** `Persistence/PersonWire26.cs` sets `LatestDevelopmentSchema` to writer 28 and adds the section to `PersonProperty26`, the omit list every frozen pre-26 codec uses. F5e changed the same constant for its bump.
- **Unchanged.** No dialog, billing, survey runner or Runtime attach. `MarketResearchSettings.Available` stays false. `SaveEpochStore.PublishedKeys` is unchanged, because the section lives inside `persistentcity:state_v1`. No migration.

## Wire

`"Research":[1, NextSequence, [open], [kept], [closed]]`. An order is `[1, sequence, product code, price units, business, agency, tax, radius, order minute, survey minute, state, decline reason, report ID, report | null]`. A report is `[1, query key, tier, logical day, address | null, radius, [[metric, band, value | null], ...]]`. Lists keep the ledger's own order. Older writers and the frozen readers omit the field. The writer-28 reader refuses it as an unknown field.

## Reader and writer rules

Before allocating anything the reader checks canonical integer and money tokens, exact strings and the version slot of every tuple. It also checks the bounds: at most 8 open, 16 kept and 16 closed orders, at most 64 cells per report, radius at most 2,000, cell values from 0 to 150,000, addresses at most 256 characters. Then come the report rules that the ledger does not own. The cell count must match the tier (13, 62 or 64). Each cell must carry the C4 metric and band at its index, and cells 62 and 63 must have no value. The query key must be 44 base64 characters. The logical day must be the day of the survey minute. Last, `ResearchLedger.TryRestore` checks known products and their exact price, every state and decline reason, each state in its own list, reports only on Surveyed and Paid orders, unique sequences below `NextSequence`, open orders in sequence with one per business, and report IDs not reused. The writer runs the same rules through the same restore, so it never writes a ledger the reader would refuse. Both record counters charge one record per order.

Cases in `tests/ResearchSaveTests.cs`:

- **Next-free writer.** `ResearchSchema == WorkplaceCapacitySchema + 1 == CurrentSchema`, and `LatestDevelopmentSchema` is writer 28. Writers 28 and 27 omit the section. No test hard-codes the writer number apart from the pin file's folder and `writer` field.
- **Round trip.** A ledger holding every state (Ordered, Surveyed, Paid, Declined with both reasons, Cancelled, PaymentUnknown, and Replaced after a 17th delivery) round-trips byte for byte, field by field and cell by cell. After reload a Paid report reads through `TryRead`, a Replaced or Surveyed one does not, and the next order takes the next sequence. A fresh city writes `[1,1,[],[],[]]`. The section at its bounds (all DetailedLocal, every business, agency and report address at 256 characters) round-trips, stays under 64 KB with an allowance for 6-digit cell values, and both record counters agree.
- **Strict refusal** (56 mutations, each refused as Invalid with the payload and the other keys kept through codec, store and session). Covered: an unknown product code, unknown or negative states and decline reasons, a state in the wrong list, a decline reason that doesn't fit the state, cells over 64, a cell count that doesn't match the tier, an unknown metric or band, a cell off the C4 layout, cell values out of range or not integers, more than 8 open, 16 kept or 16 closed orders, `NextSequence` of 0, duplicate sequences across lists, a sequence reused at or above `NextSequence`, open orders out of sequence, two open orders for one business, duplicate report IDs, a price that differs from the product's or is a fraction or text, out-of-range radii, a city radius that is not 0, a survey minute that is not the accounting target, a negative minute, address bounds, a tax flag given as an integer, reports where the state forbids them or missing where it needs them, malformed report IDs, a report tier, day, address or radius that doesn't match its order, unknown versions, and surplus or missing slots.
- **Non-canonical numbers** (10 cases): `500.0`, leading zeros, exponent form, `+` and hexadecimal in price, sequence, radius, cell and `NextSequence` are all refused and kept. A missing, null, object-shaped or duplicated `Research` field is refused and kept.
- **Writer refusal.** A ledger holding a report C4 could not produce (bad query key) fails `Write` and `ValidateForActivation`, and `SaveStore.WriteTo` writes nothing. A null ledger fails `Write`.
- **Older development city.** A writer-28 city (frozen `DevelopmentChain.Write28`) returns `DevelopmentSchema` with "start a new city" from codec, store, session and the early capability probe. Every key stays unchanged, and the frozen writer-28 reader still loads it. A current payload relabelled 28 is refused the same way. A future label is refused as Future and kept.
- **Reader-corpus entry.** `tests/Writer29/refused-writer-28-minimal.json` holds frozen writer-28 bytes, pinned by SHA-256. They are the same bytes as F5e's pinned `current-schema-minimal` fixture. The current reader refuses them as an older development city and keeps them byte for byte.
- **Golden pins.** `tests/Writer29/golden.json` pins four fixtures: the minimal city, the all-states ledger, the section at its bounds, and F5e's banded visitor city after 30 hours with every state. The banded city writes exactly writer 28's 169,914 characters plus the section, so nothing outside the section changed. The frozen writer-28 pins in `Schema28Tests` are unchanged and now go through `Write28`/`ReadWriter28`.
- **Cell layout.** The codec's layout table equals the engine's own Basic, Detailed and In-depth reports, index by index.

Changes to other tests: the literal writer-28 checks in `ActiveJourneyIndexTests`, `PopulationCensusTests`, `PersonIdentityTests`, `FoodFeedbackTests`, `SaveCostDiagnosticsTests` and `Schema18ReviewTests` now follow the current schema, and schema 28 joins the refused lists (`CurrentSchemaOnlyTests` too). The C4 guard in `MarketResearchTests` used to require no research member on `CityState`. It now requires the C5 ledger to be the only one, so no engine, cursor or report becomes a city member.
