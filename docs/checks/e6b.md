## What the code does

- **Bridge.** `StreetFoodBridge.Open(assembly)` gives "Street Food is not loaded; Street Food vendor API missing." without the assembly, "Street Food vendor API missing." without the type, and the bind error otherwise, for example "Street Food VendorApi: unsupported contract 2; Persistent City reads contract 1." There is no fallback into private members. A member that throws is a contract fault. The revision is read through a typed delegate (no allocation).
- **Image.** On a changed revision PC calls `Read` once and copies at most 16 vehicles per unit into the spare image, then validates the whole image and swaps. A broken vehicle (bad id, kind, opening, times, position, rate, menu arrays, duplicate ids or items, price ≤ 0, units < 0) refuses the whole image as `Failed` with the reason. `Off`, `NotYet`, `Refused` (more than 64) and `Failed` publish an empty image with a reason; such an image is read again every 15 native minutes even without a new revision. Nothing is retired and nothing is served while the image is not Ready.
- **Whole-vehicle rule.** Every item canonical and executable (the original three until E5b), 1-8 items, no `MenuOverflow`. Otherwise the vehicle gets no row and the reason, for example "menu item not executable: ba:itemname_kabob", is listed in `Refusals`. Units of zero are sold out, not a refusal.
- **Ownership.** An eligible vehicle is Desired and PC calls `RequestOwner(true)` (renewing the lease first), recording the native hour. A grant is accepted only if the opening's hour is later than that hour, the opening was crew-opened, is not an event or catering opening, has a planned close less than a day later, and the sampler finds a walkable point within 2 m. Otherwise PC calls `RequestOwner(false)` at once (no PC work exists for that opening) and requests again for the next one once Street Food has confirmed the handback. A handback that returns another status or throws stays pending (`VendorAuthority.HandbackPending`) and is retried every 5 native minutes; nothing is requested or acquired for that vehicle meanwhile. `FromHour` is the accepted opening's hour. When the vehicle stops being eligible, Desired clears at once (no new service); `RequestOwner(false)` and `UntilHour` follow only after no meal, excursion, held decision or routine stop names its place.
- **Lease.** Renewed once in every 15-minute slot while any row wants or owns a vehicle. A lapsed lease (PC stopped) means Street Food took the openings back; PC notices the handback in the snapshot, closes the place and requests again. `ReleaseAll` hands everything back and stops the coordinator.
- **Serve.** `CanServe` is true only while the image is Ready, the lease is valid, the snapshot shows the vehicle open, owned by PC, at the accepted opening, the stand is published and not waiting to move, `MealsPerHour > 0`, and both rules still hold.
- **Stands and places.** The vendor place is a walking-only `PlaceKind.Shop` place in the simulator's place map, never in `CityState.Places`, with one weekly window for the opening and no hours otherwise. Its Vehicle scope (`AlternativeKind.Player`, products = the menu) is created with the first published stand. A new opening at another spot (more than 0.1 m away) while anything short-lived names the place is deferred: the place keeps the old position with no hours, and the move applies at the next check after those references clear (checks run at most every 5 native minutes while something waits). Physical residents whose place or destination is the vendor place also pin it, so a return route never starts at a moved stand. Every publish invalidates the shared awareness image; the existing staged builder rebuilds it.
- **Retirement.** A vehicle missing from a Ready image retires at once (live + retired unchanged), before newcomers are admitted. `Compact` removes a retired row whose place nothing names (no meal in any non-final or uncertain state, excursion, held decision, routine stop, history row, or pending receipt reported by the caller); if a memory business reference names the place it becomes a tombstone (id, generation, place, business only; place and scope removed), or stays retired if 1,024 tombstones exist. A returning retired or tombstoned vehicle becomes live with the next generation at the same place id; a retired one waits until nothing short-lived names its place, so residents committed to the old generation resolve against the old business id. A vehicle reporting another kind is released after the drain and then becomes the next generation.
- **Capacity.** Live ≤ 64, live + retired ≤ 128, tombstones ≤ 1,024, checked after every transition. A newcomer is refused with "vendor table full: 64 live vehicles" or "vendor table full: 128 live and retired vehicles".

## Technical decisions

1. **Core port.** The bridge implements a Core interface `IVendorApi`, so all ownership, lease and stand rules are pure Core and tested against the real bridge over the stand-in.
2. **Channel not saved yet.** `FoodScope.Channel` has a non-public setter (`UseChannel` sets it), so the current writer ignores it and the writer-26 golden hash is unchanged. E6c serializes it. Consequence: a city holding a Vehicle scope is not saveable until E6c, which is fine because nothing creates one in the game build.
3. **Player-opened and event openings** are handed back when granted, because the contract grants per opening and PC cannot refuse an opening in advance. Street Food then runs its own queue for that opening from the same minute.
4. **Same spot is not a move.** A reopening within 0.1 m (the codec's `Same` distance) publishes at once even with committed residents.
5. **No vehicle scope in a market that cannot tell channels.** The pre-awareness shortlist and the awareness sample offer a Vehicle scope only through `IFoodCandidateMarket.IsCandidate`, which E6d's composite routes by channel.
6. **Vendor rows live outside `CityState` for now.** The coordinator takes a `VendorTable`; E6c adds it to the population payload.
