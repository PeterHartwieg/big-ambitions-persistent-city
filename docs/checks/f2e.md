## What is implemented

- **`CitySimulator.HoldForPlayer(citizenId, blockedMinutes, cause)`** (`Core/PlayerHolds.cs`). The player blocked this person for the last `blockedMinutes`, in (0, 0.25]. Core applies the hold inside the call. Nothing about it survives the call except the person's consumed allowance and the city's hold sequence, both saved.
  - Walk leg: `DepartureMinute` and `NextEventMinute` move later by the applied minutes and the due event is rescheduled, so progress is what it was at the start of the blocked interval. The applied value never takes progress below the start of the current walk leg.
  - Traffic leg: `TrafficKernel.HoldVehicle` (below), then the person's published departure follows the kernel's rebased coordinate.
  - Allowance: at most 1 game minute per journey (`PlayerHolds.MaximumMinutesPerJourney`). The remaining allowance clamps the input and bounds the charge: a car hold that would miss a signal window and charge more than is left is shortened until it fits (`HoldVehicle`'s `maximumCharge`, review Sol-r1-1), so the saved minutes always equal the sum of the logged charges. `CitizenState.PlayerHoldMinutes` holds what is used. `CommitEvent` clears it when the journey ends or is replaced, so reloading during a blockage cannot reset it.
  - Refused, with nothing changed and nothing logged: invalid input, unknown person, visitor (no body before F5i), not travelling (arrived), a minute that has not drained, allowance spent, wrong leg (a foot hold on a car, transit or pause leg, a vehicle hold without a kernel vehicle), already at the leg start, a waiting source with no link, and a traffic hold that changes nothing.
- **`TrafficKernel.HoldVehicle(id, minutes)`** (`Core/TrafficKernel.cs`). All bounds are in the held leg's motion coordinate u: new u = min(u_now, max(u_motion, u_entry, u_floor, u_follower)). u_motion is the motion model at `Clock − minutes`, u_entry = 0, u_floor comes from `NominalMinutes`, and u_follower is the follower's sampled spatial fraction plus one storage spacing, converted to u. Then `MotionStartFraction = u`, `MotionStartedAt = Clock`, `ReadyAt = max(old, Clock + (1 − u) × FreeFlowMinutes)`, and `Reconsider` reschedules the link. The charge is the change of the vehicle's own earliest discharge (`EligibleAt`, or `CurbExitAt` for a curb exit). Headway or a signal can absorb part of the input. A hold that changes neither motion nor discharge is refused and restores every field. A hold that changes motion with a charge of 0 is applied. Snapshot permissions and `CurbExitAt` read the rebased motion, so followers gain no space and no curb-exit permission.
- **Saved state, schema 35** (`CityState.PlayerHoldSchema`). `CitizenState.PlayerHoldMinutes` and `CityState.PlayerHoldSequence`, in the same compact wire as F7d. Older development cities, writer 34 included, are refused with the distinct "start a new city" status and kept byte for byte. The reader validates both fields: minutes in [0, 1] and zero unless travelling; sequence ≥ 0.
- **Hold stream.** Each applied hold takes the next `PlayerHoldSequence` and is one Player.log line: `[Persistent City] hold seq=… minute=… person=… cause=player-foot|player-vehicle input=… charge=…`, plus `motionStart=… motionAt=… readyAt=…` for a car. Doubles are written with `G17`, which round-trips on every runtime, and the person ID is URI-escaped. `PlayerHoldRing` keeps the last 256 applied (and replayed) holds of the loaded city for `pc movement holds since <seq>`; a load or save change starts it empty, a presentation rebuild over the same state keeps it. That command prints `gap`, `oldest_sequence`, `last_sequence`, `city_sequence`, the contact counters, `count` and the lines, each with the prefix `[Persistent City] hold readback seq=`, which the stream parser never reads as an original.
- **Replay.** `PlayerHolds.ValidateSegment` refuses a missing, duplicated or out-of-order sequence number, minutes that go back, and a segment that does not start at the starting checkpoint's sequence or end at the end checkpoint's. `PlayerHoldReplay` stops each advance on the next stamped minute (`Limit`), waits until that minute has drained, then calls `HoldForPlayer` with the recorded input. It refuses on any difference in sequence, cause, person, charge or the three motion doubles, compared bit for bit.
- **`pc movement replay-holds <file>`** (`Runtime/MovementDiagnostics.cs`). It reads the original hold lines from a file (replay and readback copies and every other line are ignored) and exactly one required `segment start=<seq> end=<seq>` line with the two checkpoints' sequences (`PlayerHolds.ReadSegment`), so a missing tail or an empty file is refused. The path cannot contain spaces, because the console splits arguments on them. It refuses unless bodies are off (`PhysicalEnabled = false`) and the loaded city's saved sequence is the segment start. While it runs, `PersistentCityMod` caps each frame's advance at the next stamped minute and applies due holds after the advance. Those are two lines at the `AdvancePopulation` call, inactive unless a replay is running. Replayed holds are logged as `[Persistent City] hold replay seq=…`, which the stream parser never reads as an original. The command reports `hold replay complete` or `hold replay refused: <reason>`.
- **Contact detection** (`Runtime/PlayerContact.cs`). A following body is blocked when, since the previous frame, its logical pose moved on while its own progress stood still or its lag grew. Contact is that plus the player's avatar within 1 m ahead of a walker (along the walker's own leg), or the player's vehicle within 4 m ahead of a car on its current road segment (projection inside the segment, within 2.5 m laterally). The pose comes only from `GameManager.playerController` or, when `GameManager.IsDrivingVehicle()`, `GameManager.selectedVehicle`. Native pedestrians, RLO actors and service vehicles are never read, so they cannot cause a hold. Inside a building view nobody is held. Blocked game minutes accumulate per person. A report is made every 0.05 game minutes and once more when the contact ends, at most 0.25 and at most the time since that person's previous report. A refusal for an undrained minute keeps the minutes for the next frame. After `AllowanceSpent` that journey is not reported again.
- **The one call site.** `PhysicalPopulation.Update` reads the player's pose once per frame and runs `ObservePlayerContact` for each kept binding, after its follow step. That method holds the only `simulation.HoldForPlayer` call in Runtime. The F2b source test now allows exactly `HoldForPlayer` besides the read-only list and asserts one call site in all of Runtime. The walker and car follow regions it pins stay read-only.
- **E2 arm** (`tools/movement-equivalence/HoldReplay.cs`). `MovementOptions.PlayerContact` adds a player double: inside 07:00–10:00 it blocks every third followed body (by hash of the person) and reports with the Runtime rule. Every applied hold is written as its stream line and parsed back, so the replay reads the logged stream. `MovementOptions.Checkpoints` keeps the saved city, the checkout double's state, the native clock and the saved sequence where the native clock enters each listed minute. `HoldReplay.Replay` loads a checkpoint with bodies off and runs the harness frame loop under the F1 fence with `PlayerHoldReplay`. The bytes where the native clock enters the end checkpoint's minute must equal that checkpoint's. The tool's self-check section runs the E2 rows (`HoldReplay.Check`).

### Technical choices

- **Holds apply only at a drained minute** (`IsCaughtUpTo(State.Minute)`, no pending plan, kernel clock equal to the city's). That makes replay exact: a replay that advances to the stamped minute reaches the same state the live run had. Runtime retries next frame.
- **"At most the time since the previous report" is Runtime's rule** (`PlayerContact`). Core stays stateless between calls, as Decision 4 requires, and enforces only (0, 0.25] and the allowance.
- **The traffic handoff clamp.** Road sections are car legs, so a completed traffic leg ends at or before the start of any later walk leg. The walk floor, the current walk leg's start, therefore never undoes a handoff. A foot hold on the kerb connector or parking pause after traffic is refused as the wrong leg. On the car side u_entry = 0 keeps a link transition.
- **u_now is the motion coordinate**, not the queue-limited sampled position. A car its queue already holds back can still be held: its motion and readiness move back, its sampled pose stays where the queue keeps it.
- **Follower spacing is exact.** Converting the follower's spacing into u can round one ulp behind it. `HoldVehicle` steps u forward (at most 64 ulps, then u_now) until the follower's sampled position is unchanged.
- **A hold without a motion change leaves the motion anchor untouched.** Only readiness may move, and that counts only when it moves the discharge. A car standing at a red light longer than the report is therefore refused.
- **Cause must match the leg.** Foot holds walkers on walk legs only; vehicle holds kernel cars only.
- **Compact wire at 35.** The writer writes its own schema in the envelope, so `DevelopmentChain.Write34` in tests still produces the frozen F7d bytes. The pinned writer-34 hashes are unchanged, which shows F2e changed nothing else in the shape. The decoder accepts a 34 or 35 envelope. `Read` reaches it only through the current writer's prefix, and the body reader then requires schema 35, so a writer-34 city is refused as an older development city. The frozen pre-31 codecs omit the new fields through `EconomicProperty`, as F6g did for `StockoutObserved`.
- **Native player members.** `GameManager.IsDrivingVehicle()`, `GameManager.selectedVehicle` (a `VehicleController`) and `playerController.transform` compile against the reference assemblies. That `selectedVehicle` is the vehicle the player is driving is an assumption that rig check 9 tests.
- **Files outside the owned list,** each needed for the bump or the commands: `Core/CitySimulator.cs` (clone and commit of the allowance), `Persistence/SaveSnapshot.cs` (copy of both fields), `Persistence/CompactWire34.cs`, `Persistence/PopulationWire18.cs`, `Persistence/EconomicLedgerWire.cs`, `Runtime/MovementDiagnostics.cs` (the two commands), `Runtime/PersistentCityMod.cs` (two lines), the harness's `PresentationDriver.Followed`, `MovementMarket.Copy` and `Program.cs`, the test project links and native doubles (`tests/PlayerHoldDoubles.cs`), the reader corpus `schema-35` folder, and the existing tests that pinned 34 as the current writer. No food-contract, E5b4/E5b5, F2g or F5i file is touched.
