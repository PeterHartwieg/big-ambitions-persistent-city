# E5b3: contract coordinator, reconciliation and dirty recovery

Slice row: [E5b remainder plan, section 4](../plans/e5b-remaining.md#4-slice-breakdown), Decisions 3, 3a, 4 to 8 and 13. Base `origin/codex/persistent-city` at `ece4878` (F2d #169), with E5b2 (#168) merged. Current writer 34. No schema change, and the writer-34 golden hash is unchanged (`F5hIntegratedAcceptanceTests.CurrentWriterGoldenHashIsUnchanged` passes). Stamps, images, queues and refusals are Runtime memory rebuilt at load. Nothing is saved, and `FoodAuthority.Deferred` is never written (`DeferredStateIsNeverSavedAndTheE5b1BoundaryIsUnchanged`).

## What changed

The original-three guard still decides which products a shop may sell. `SupportedBusiness` checks `FoodProducts.Known`, `MenuStatus` returns `DynamicIntegrationRefusal` for any other product, and the live width stays at 4. A source test pins all three. What changes is that original-three shops now get the stricter E5b contract (Decision 13).

### Core (pure, tested against doubles)

- `Core/FoodContractState.cs` (new).
  - `FoodContractImage` is one immutable published image: the E5a eligibility image plus a `FoodHolderSlot` per contract holder (instance, cargo list by reference, version and count, stock slot by reference and name, nested count), a `FoodDefinitionDependency` per definition used (live reference plus catalogue copy), a `FoodBusinessTypeCopy`, the Decision 8 admission result and the start stamp. A Q2 price change publishes a new object through `WithPrices`.
  - `FoodContractRecheck` holds the Decision 3 checks: `Holders`, `Definitions` (content, element by element), `BusinessType` and `Prices`. The prices rule is one row per product with an exact positive price that `Money.TryFromNative` accepts. A different valid price is `PriceOnly`, never a refusal. `StaleCopies` compares live content with the catalogue's current copy at preparation. `FoodDefinitionEpoch` compares each distinct copy once per capture batch, checkout, preparation hour or boundary.
  - `FoodHolderReconciliation.Check` is Decision 5. Every native producer must be an image holder for that product by instance reference, and none may be an `EmployeeWorkstation`. Every native point of sale must be an image bag counter. An image holder with positive live stock that native setup left out is `OmittedPositiveHolder` (unknown, not zero). A per-image reference index keeps it O(n).
  - `FoodContractCoordinator` keeps per-shop state, with phases Clean, Dirty, Rebuilding, Published, Refused and Recovering, each derived from the held image, the pending flags and the recorded refusal. It also holds the coalesced preparation queue, `Ready` (Decision 4 step 4), the 16-per-boundary commit budget, `RecoveryGate`, and the recovery queue (at most 64 units per frame, each shop at most once per accounting hour). Bounded at 1,024 shops.
  - `FoodContractBoundary.Commit` runs Decision 4 for one drained row: readiness, then budget, then the gate, then `FoodAuthority.Boundary`. `Order` sorts by `RequestedHour`, then place ID.
- `Core/FoodAuthority.cs`: `Pending` (what a boundary would do, without mutating) and a `Boundary` overload with a `contractReady` input. An acquisition that is not ready sets `Deferred` and leaves `Desired` and `RequestedHour` untouched. A hand-back never waits for readiness. The four-argument `Boundary` is the five-argument one with `true`, and a matrix test pins that.
- `Core/LoadedStockEligibility.cs`: `LoadedStockFacts` (live fields, uncopied) and `LoadedStockDefinition.Matches`. The comparison covers type flags, verified/ungated, bag/counter, consumes, `itemsThatCanShowcase` and `itemsToProduce` element by element, tags and `itemsWhitelist` as count plus an ordinal FNV-1a hash, and `cargoCapacity`. `LoadedStockCatalogue.WithReplacements` is the per-definition re-copy: a new sealed revision that shares the table and overrides the named rows, up to 256 per chain.
- `Core/CompleteFoodMenu.cs`: `HistoryProducts` (today's history row for the business) and `FoodReferencedNames`, a superset of the saved name table. It is seeded from the same sources the save writer reads (scopes, residents, history, economy ledger) and grows with every scope product.

### Runtime

- `Runtime/FoodContractDiagnostics.cs` drives the coordinator.
  - At start it binds and self-tests a `List<CargoInstance>._version` reader, then seeds every discovered shop. It starts in Warming mode, where the E5b1 contract serves and acquisitions are deferred, until the catalogue and every seeded shop have an image. Then it switches to Active.
  - A capability failure, a shadow fault or a refused catalogue leaves the market in Fallback, which is the E5b1 contract unchanged. `pc food contract status` names the reason.
  - Per frame: preparation units, recovery units and reader units share a 0.5 ms budget. The loop stops before a unit that would overrun it, and recovery units skipped by the budget go back to the queue.
  - `OnContractPublished` turns a finished reader pass into a contract image. It discards the pass if the stamp moved, a holder lost its shape or a definition went missing. If a definition's content changed during the pass, it re-copies the definition and lets the shop rebuild.
- `Runtime/NativeFoodMarket.cs`: `SupportedMenu` also needs the published image plus a matching stamp. Captures and checkouts run the Decision 3 recheck (checkout again after native setup and before the receipt). Pre-sale supply reads pass the image to `FoodSupplyReader`. `RefuseContract` records the refused hour in the coordinator too.
- `Runtime/NativeFoodCandidates.cs`: a registration-to-place index, so `SupportedMenu` reads the image without building a place ID. At each advancing hour it queues preparation for owned, acquiring and refused shops. E5b1's `RevalidateRefusedContract` now runs only in Fallback. A capture batch starts a definition epoch.
- `Runtime/NativeFoodOwnership.cs`: the drained boundary sorts rows and commits through `CommitBoundary`. `Row` gives a side-effect-free lookup and `LiveNativeCustomers` a live customer check.
- `Runtime/FoodSupplyReader.cs`: `ReadMenu(..., FoodContractImage?)` reconciles after the setup it already makes. A mismatch leaves every product unverified, which every caller already treats as a whole-business refusal.
- `Runtime/LoadedCatalogueEligibilityReader.cs` and `Runtime/FoodContractStamps.cs` (E5b2 files, outside the listed set) got additive edits only. `Facts(Item)` is now the one function behind both the catalogue copy and every recheck. Tags are read through a `DynamicMethod`. The other additions are `CurrentStamp`, the `ShopPublished` callback, `RecopyDefinitions`, `RequeueAllShops` and `LoadedCatalogueRevision.Replace`, which republishes the name image with the same names. Stamps, hooks, the name image and the shadow reader behave as before. Putting re-copy anywhere else would have meant duplicating the reader's definition copy.

## How each decision is met

| Decision | Where |
| --- | --- |
| 1 and 2 at `SupportedMenu` | `ContractAdmits`: a stamp mismatch drops the image and records a contract refusal (`RefuseStructural`), and the visit's `Observe(reg, false)` withdraws desire in the same visit. A passing same-hour rebuild cannot restore service; only recovery can. A catalogue revision change reaches service at once: the image stays admitted only if every dependency is unchanged in the current catalogue (`CatalogueCurrent`, then `Rebase`), otherwise the shop is refused. While the catalogue rebuilds, no capture or sale runs and acquisitions are not ready. |
| 3 | `ContractRecheck` before a capture's supply read, at checkout, and again after native setup. A failure refuses the contract, records the refusal and withdraws. |
| 3a | `Definitions` at every recheck, at preparation and at the gate. Re-copy goes through `RecopyDefinitions`. A business-type change re-caches the bag policy and rebuilds every shop. `StaleCopies` covers failed images. |
| 4 | Prepare: `PrepareContractHour`, then `PrepareUnit` (3a first, then the reader pass). Serve: the held image keeps serving while its stamp matches. Publish: one `contracts.Publish` swap. A failed image withdraws at once. Commit: `CommitBoundary` and `CommitGate` after `ForceCompleteAllWork`. |
| 5 | `FoodSupplyReader.ReadMenu` with the image, at capture, checkout, decision reads and recovery. No new `SetUp` call site; a source test pins the count at 2. |
| 6 | No quantity is compared or hinted. Known zero stays zero, and an omitted positive holder is unknown. |
| 7 | Refused shops are prepared every hour. A passing image built after the refused hour queues one recovery unit. The gates are an advancing hour, a fresh image (built after the refusal and no older than the hour before), a completed hand-back, no live native customers, the matching stamp, the recheck and a reconciled supply read. |
| 8 | `Admission` calls `CompleteFoodMenu.Check` with today's history products and the name superset, at publish and again at the gate. `nativeContract` is true only after Decisions 2, 3 and 3a passed for that image. |
| 13 | `SupportedBusiness` and `DynamicIntegrationRefusal` are unchanged and pinned by `AcquisitionStaysGatedByTheOriginalThreeAndTheDynamicRefusalIsUnchanged`. |

## `pc food contract boundary`

FT cities only, read-only. It shows the mode, the current hour, the next boundary, the catalogue revision, the budgets and the queue lengths. Then it lists, per coordinator shop:

- phase
- `preparedHour` (the held image's build hour), whether it passed, and the refusal
- `refusedHour`
- `nextBoundary`: owned, hand back, native, or the readiness it would have at the next boundary (Ready, NoImage, Failed, Stale, Catalogue, Refused)
- `deferred`, `desired`, `requestedHour` and `managed`
- the last boundary hour and its readiness, including Budget and Gate

`status` gains the coordinator counters: publishes, invalidations, refusals, recoveries, deferrals, commits, discards, re-copies, recheck and gate counts with max times, reconciliation refusals, definitions compared and shared, native setups, and unit p95/max and frame max.

## Review round 1 fixes

- **Every definition a pass reads is a dependency.** The reader records menu products, every furniture definition and every cargo name the pass reads, participating or not, including the one that refused. Images depend on all of them. The coordinator keeps a watched list per shop across invalidation and failed images, and preparation re-copies from it. An unused workstation edited in place to produce a menu product is now caught at the next hour, and a shelf definition broken alongside a structural change is still watched for its repair.
- **Failed images of candidate shops are refusals.** Any original-three food shop, owned or not, gets a recorded refusal when its image fails. It is then prepared every hour, so an in-place repair such as a zero price set to 5 reaches recovery.
- **Structural changes go through recovery.** See Decision 2 above.
- **Bag policy refresh.** Every preparation unit compares the business type's live paper-bag tag with the cached policy and re-caches it on a difference, including for failed images. A tag removed and restored in place no longer leaves the policy false until reload.
- **Catalogue revision.** See Decision 1 above. The commit gate's Decision 8 check uses the rebased revision, so re-copying an unrelated definition does not stall acquisitions.
- **Registration replacement.** A replaced registration at the same address resets the coordinator entry: image, watched definitions and place-level refusal belong to the old lifetime. `FoodAuthority`'s generation and drain rules handle ownership.
- **Checkout reconciliation.** A reconciliation mismatch in the checkout's own live read refuses the contract before the receipt (`RefuseCheckoutContract`).
- **Capture epoch.** A capture outside a batch (`ReadFailureBusiness`) starts its own definition epoch.

## Known limits

- **Warming.** After a load, owned shops serve on the E5b1 contract and acquisitions are deferred until every seeded shop has an image. Offline, 1,024 shops take about 30 frames. Rig check 5 measures it in game.
- **Fallback.** If a Decision 12 capability fails, the shadow faults or the catalogue is refused, the market keeps the E5b1 contract for original-three shops instead of refusing them. E5b1 was the reviewed contract for these products.
- **More than 1,024 player shops.** Above the E5a bound, extra shops never get an image and are not admitted while the coordinator is active.
- **Dependency bound.** A pass records at most 512 distinct definition names, and a shop's watched list keeps at most 512.
- **Undrained work at recovery.** No per-shop native work counter is read. The gate is the completed hand-back (`!Managed && !OwnsHour`), which happens only after `ForceCompleteAllWork` at its boundary.
- **Hash comparison.** Tag lists and `itemsWhitelist` are compared as count plus a 64-bit FNV-1a hash. The bag, counter and verified facts derived from tags are compared exactly.
- **Tools.** `tools/e5a-eligibility-regression/source_checks.py` already failed after E5b2 (it asserts no production caller). The `tools/*-regression` suites are not run before release.
- **Shared docs.** This slice does not edit CHECKS.md or the roadmap. The coordinator updates them after merge (AGENTS.md).
