## What changed

- `Runtime/HookConflicts.cs`: the policy table `CheckoutPolicy` (the five H1 customer targets and H2). `Scan` builds the report with it. `ScanForLoad` keeps the load's scan; `TakeLoadScan` hands it to the activation line once.
- `Core/HookConflictReport.cs`: on a policy target, the first foreign owner that is not an observer gives `foreign <kind> by <owner> on <target>`. `pc capability hooks` marks that entry `-> refuses food.checkout.shop`; the activation line names the refusal.
- `Runtime/NativeFoodContract.cs`: `CheckoutForLoad` combines the member contract (first) with the conflict refusal. A scan that throws refuses checkout with `hook conflict scan failed: <message>` (fail closed: an unchecked hook takes over no shop).
- `Runtime/NativeFoodMarket.cs` and the `RefreshDiscovery` line in `Runtime/NativeFoodCandidates.cs`: the decision waits for the game's city load to finish (`LoadScene.isLoading`), because another mod may install its patches during the load. A market built while loading uses the member contract for eligibility, sells nothing (`CanServe` false, `BuyLive` returns before any receipt) and installs no price prefix. Every scoped shop reports `AwaitingValidation` meanwhile, so Core holds saved travelling and serving meals (neither bought nor missed) until the decision serves or withdraws the shop. The first `RefreshDiscovery` after the load scans and decides once. A refusal uses F8c's checkout-off mode unchanged: no price prefix, no shop served, `ProcessCustomer` not resolved, every shop observed ineligible, owned shops handed back at the next drained boundary. A market built after the load decides in its constructor as before.
- `Runtime/PersistentCityMod.cs`, one line: `CompleteInitialization` calls `LogActivationLines()` in place of the two activation log lines. `Runtime/CapabilityDiagnostics.cs` writes them at once when checkout is decided, or when a deferred decision is made, from the deciding scan, so the lines show the final `food.checkout.shop` state and a load scans once.
- `Runtime/CapabilityDiagnostics.cs`: the activation line reuses the deciding scan when there is one. `pc capability hooks` shows the live scan's marks; when they differ from the attached market's load decision, a closing line says `food.checkout.shop at this load: ...; the marks above are the live scan and apply at the next load.` `pc capability status` always shows the load decision.
